Reports, PR 2 of 2: a Report button next to Block on a room seat, beside each person in match history and on a public profile, with four closed reasons (offensive name, cheating or stalling, harassment through emotes or requests, something else) and an optional note of up to 500 characters with emails and phone numbers masked as feedback is. `POST /api/reports` takes a room seat, a match seat or a profile handle, never a player id, and answers one 404 for anyone the server cannot confirm; the server keeps who was reported, the name then and the last 10 moves and 5 requests between the two, named only by role. The reporter gets a neutral thank-you with "Block too" in one tap (`POST /api/blocks` with `{ reportId }`), and the reported player is never told. Limits: one report per reporter and player per India day (unique index), 10 a day per player, 30 per address counted after the player, and reporters whose reports were dismissed are set aside, never acted on by counts alone. The console's new Reports tab (`/admin/reports`, the allowlist and the same 404) groups open reports by player without any id, counts reasons and reporters, shows notes as text, and can dismiss a group or rename an account to `Player_` and six letters, which also renames its leaderboard rows and signs it out everywhere. A new `player_reports` store kept 180 days by a TTL index; reports move with a guest who signs in, the reporter's own are in their export without the other player's id, and deletion removes reports about the player and keeps the player's own without their id or note. DATA.md and the privacy page list it. Account suspension does not exist yet and is a follow-up.
