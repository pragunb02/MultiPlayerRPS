Quick match, behind the new `quick_match` flag (off, everyone, remove by 2027-01-15). With it on, "Play Rocky" (and
Pip and Queen Vee) first looks for a person for up to 4 seconds and says so before the press; the character's room
is made ready at the same time, so when nobody is free the character starts in it with no wait after the search,
and a "Nobody was free just now" line says why. A person found joins the older search's room and both start at
once. An empty site skips the search (ADR-029), and after 20 matched searches the search lasts their 80th
percentile wait, at most 4 s. One in-memory pool per game and per 300-point chess band (one server only until
CC-061): first come first served, chess within 100 points widening to 300, at 3+2. Never paired: the same person, a
blocked pair either way (checked again as the person sits down), a pair paired 10 times in 24 hours. Cancel,
leaving or hiding the page and closing the tab end a search at once, and every search ends exactly once
(`person`, `character` or `cancelled`), races included. Minors play under the no-contact rules: the room is marked
`quickMatch`, so no friend request or challenge again follows from it, and a new age policy field can keep any
audience out. Anonymous counts per India day, hour, game and outcome, waits in buckets, are logged once a minute.
No player id reaches a client. Decisions by the design lead, owner-delegated 2026-10-21 (ADR-079).
