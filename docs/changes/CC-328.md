The effects stack of ADR-075, built (Wave 2a of the redesign, merged before Diwali). One effect registry (`apps/web/src/effects/registry.js`, the only part on the first route), one `EffectHost` in the layout that owns the canvas and the queue, and `useEffects().play(name)` as the only way a component starts an effect: one standout effect at a time, a second waiting or dropped if over 2 s late, nothing under reduced motion, in a hidden tab or during a game, and everything cleaned up on unmount. Our own canvas particle module (one typed-array pool, at most 300 particles, a third on low-end phones, DPR at most 2) draws three celebrations for CC-327 and CC-099 to call: a big win, an achievement and a level up. Event themes are registry entries with a flag, an India-day window and a removeBy date; a theme may change only decoration tokens (a test refuses anything else and re-checks every contrast pair), adds a still ambient band and one entrance burst per visit on home, and no component knows a theme by name. The Diwali 2026 theme (warm gold, a band of diyas, a greeting on the home hero) runs 6 to 11 November behind `event_diwali_2026` and comes out by 2026-11-30; a winter theme is ready behind `event_winter_2026`, off. The console's Flags tab can preview a theme in one tab before its window opens.
