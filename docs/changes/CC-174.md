Shaped for spec review (decision-first, CC-311), answered by the owner ("Accept all", 2026-10-20) and built behind
the `telemetry_client` flag, off: the browser counts pages and landings, presses by their stable control names, dead
and rage clicks, sign-up and login form steps, errors by type only, API failures by route template, realtime drops,
the first move, how long presses wait and the page's speed, checks every event against the shared catalogue, and
sends batches every 10 seconds or 20 events and on `pagehide` by `sendBeacon` to our own server only. Nothing is held
or sent with the privacy switch off, Global Privacy Control, Do Not Track, in a browser test or on a laptop, and
nothing records that someone opted out. The client is its own file, loaded when the browser is idle after the first
paint, 3.8 KB gzipped against a 4 KB budget. Part of the owner's 2026-10-20 ask to monitor everything added to the
site without adding friction (CC-313).
