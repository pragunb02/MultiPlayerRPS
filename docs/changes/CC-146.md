Days in local time, built behind the new `local_days` flag (everyone, off, remove by 2027-01-31), so production does not change until the owner ramps it. Each player gets a calendar from their browser's time zone (`PUT /api/time-zone`, once a visit when it differs), worked out with `Intl` so summer time is right, India time when the zone is unknown. The play streak, quests and the daily puzzle follow it; analytics and the console stay on India days. A zone may change once in 7 days (409 `zone-locked` otherwise), and a change applies from the old zone's midnight and never goes back a day, so a zone change can neither replay nor skip a day. Old UTC streaks migrate on read through a `days` marker and are never broken by the switch; nothing is rewritten in bulk. The quest card and the streak card show reset times as local clock times. Once the flag is fully on, the weekly boards turn at Monday 00:00 India time (ADR-015 amended). A new collection, `player_time_zones`, is exported and erased with the account and moves with a guest (ADR-014, ADR-048).
