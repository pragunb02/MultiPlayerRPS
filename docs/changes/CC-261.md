Sign-up and password change refuse passwords attackers already know (SEC-24): first the SecLists list of the
10,000 most common passwords (MIT, bundled and credited), then Have I Been Pwned's Pwned Passwords, which gets only
the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1, padded, from the server, with a 1.5 s timeout. When it fails or is
late the password is let through after the local check. Every check is counted by outcome only, in the new
`accounts.password_checks` counter. The refusal says "That password is too easy to guess. Choose a different one."
The privacy page and DATA.md list Have I Been Pwned as a fifth service, and no test touches the network.
