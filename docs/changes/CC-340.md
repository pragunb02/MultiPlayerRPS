The load harness test "records each kind of call by name" no longer times out on a busy runner (main's CI on df8710f). Every name had come: the test hung in `swarm.stop()`, because a player that had just had an answer sent its next call on the closed socket, where Socket.IO keeps it in the send buffer until the 30 s `waitMs` runs out. A stopping swarm now makes no new call. Two slow paths found on the way are fixed too: a computer reply that overtook the answer to the move went untimed (more than half the chess replies on the test server), and the friends who rematched played Rock Paper Scissors, whose replayed draws gave the match no bound, so they now play chess and resign at once. A time-out now prints what the test was doing, the missing names and the failures. Under CPU load with coverage it failed 4 of 20 runs before and 0 of 20 after.
