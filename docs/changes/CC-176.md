Built behind the server-only `telemetry_rollup` flag, off: a job in the server process, on boot, every hour and at
00:30 India time, that rolls each complete India day's hourly usage counters into anonymous daily totals kept 13
months (`telemetry_daily`), by a rollup spec in code whose worst case is checked at 7 746 rows a day against a budget of
20 000 (busy open fields such as controls keep their top values and sum the rest). Timings get exact p50, p75 and p95
from the counters and bucket counters for reading a range. Every complete day whose hourly counters are all still kept (the last 29) with no run record is
caught up, the last 2 are always redone, a lease per day keeps a second process off it, a failed day is logged
(`telemetry rollup failed` with its step), counted (`telemetry_rollup.runs`) and retried. The report read applies
`"<5"` to every total, share and percentile. A backfill script, `scripts/bin/rollup-telemetry.mjs`, redoes a range
after a fix.
