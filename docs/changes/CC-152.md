The under 18 social defaults now live in one place. `apps/server/src/domain/safety/agePolicy.js` gives every player one policy (`canFollow`, `friendsMode`, `emotes`, `quietHours` 22:00 to 07:00 India time, `shareCardName`, `maxOwnedCircleSize` 10 for minors, `publicProfile`, `boardName` and more), with guests and accounts of unknown age treated as minors (ADR-028.1). The leaderboards, profiles, the profile cards on boards, rooms and history, feedback and the result share card read it instead of working out an age, and a test fails when any other module does. The browser gets only its own flags (`GET /api/safety`). The owner can now correct or clear a player's year of birth on request, from a new "Ages" page in the console (`PUT /api/admin/accounts/birth-year`, 404 for everyone else, the year never logged), and the export shows when. The year picker's hint, the under 13 refusal and the profile's year lines are in Hinglish and English. Nothing is migrated: accounts with no year stay "unknown means minor". Friends (CC-052) are not built yet; the hand-off is in the ticket.
