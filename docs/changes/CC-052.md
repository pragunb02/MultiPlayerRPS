Part 1 of friends: mutual friends for accounts, asked with Add friend beside a seat in a room or on the result
screen and beside a past opponent in match history, or through a one-use 7 day "be my friend" link that works well
in WhatsApp and is stored only as a hash. The other player accepts or says Not now, and the sender is never told.
A block removes the friendship both ways, and a request into a block answers "Request sent" like any other. A
friends page at `/friends` lists friends with their avatar and presence (online, in a game or offline, to friends
only, never which room) and an Appear offline switch. Guests are told to save their progress, minors befriend only
people met by a link and see a safety line, requests wait 30 days with 10 at a time and 200 friends, and every
route is rate limited per player and per address. Four new collections (`friendships`, `friend_requests`,
`friend_links`, `player_privacy`), with deletion, export, DATA.md and the privacy page. Part 2, invites, behind the
`friend_invites` flag (off, signed-in, removed by 2027-01-19, rolling out from the console): Invite beside each
friend opens a game picker and sends a challenge only that friend can take, which reaches them as the site-wide
popup, waits for the end of their game, or waits 24 hours in their inbox with Send on WhatsApp for the sender. The
friend who took an invite whose sender is away reads "isn't here right now, we've told them", and after 5 minutes
can keep it for later or play Pip meanwhile, which invites the sender back. Of several friends invited to one
game, the first to accept plays and the others see "Game filled" with Invite again, decided by one conditional
update. A decline, no answer and a block all read "Not right now", and in a minor's quiet hours an invite waits in
the inbox without a popup or a sound. Anyone who is not a friend gets one 404, with the challenge limits for
invites back too. Turn-based play is CC-222.
