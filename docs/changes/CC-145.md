Streak freezes, built behind the new `streak_freezes` flag (everyone, off, remove by 2027-01-31). The first counted match of each week earns a free freeze, at most 2 held, never bought or spent on anything else (ADR-026.5). A missed day is covered automatically at the next counted match, one freeze a day and only when the freezes cover every missed day; reading the streak spends nothing. Two counted matches on the day the streak carries on earn the freeze back, once. A frozen day keeps the streak and adds nothing to it, and the best streak never goes down. The streak card shows a small freeze indicator in the CC-314 info tokens ("1 of 2 freezes"), the next milestone (3, 7, 30, 100, 365 days) and plain copy with no loss words, checked by a test; CC-330 restyles it. Freezes live on `player_stats.dailyStreak` (old documents read as none), move with a guest as the larger holding with the covered days kept for the rebuild, and are in the export.
