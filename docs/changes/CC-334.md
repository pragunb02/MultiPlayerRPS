Account suspension, the follow-up from CC-044 part 2: from a group of `/admin/reports` the owner suspends the reported account for 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or until lifted, with a reason from a closed list and no free text, by the report's id (`POST /api/admin/reports/:reportId/suspension`). Every session of the account ends at once through its session version and its sockets are cut; it leaves its open rooms as any player leaves (a running game is forfeited); its open challenges, waiting friend requests and friend links end; friends see it offline; its leaderboard rows are hidden while it lasts and ranks close up. Nothing is deleted. Signing in with the right password answers 403 with the end date only, and the page says when the pause ends and offers the feedback form as the appeal; a wrong password is the usual 401. A suspended account downloads its data with its email and password (`POST /api/auth/suspended-export`). The console shows each group's history and a "Suspended now" list with Lift, admins named by name, never an id. A new `account_suspensions` store, kept 180 days after the suspension ends or is lifted by a TTL, removed with the account, and listed in the export without who acted. Reporters are never told, the suspended player never learns who reported, and no answer carries a player id (a `hasPlayerIdShape` crawl). Guests cannot be suspended: the gap and the address-level options are written down, IP bans are not built. Admin accounts cannot be suspended. No flag: a safety tool only the owner uses. DATA.md and the privacy page list the store.
