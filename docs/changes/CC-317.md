The browser test "Back focuses the heading and keeps the place the browser scrolled back to" no longer fails on a busy runner. It went Back as soon as the URL said `/privacy`, but React Router draws the new page as a transition, and on a slow phone Back replaced it before it was ever shown, so the app (rightly) left focus alone. The test now waits for the privacy page's focused heading before going Back. With the CPU slowed 30 times from the tap to Back it failed 40 of 40 runs before and passes 40 of 40 now. The app does not change.
