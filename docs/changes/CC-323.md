The shared components every page uses, built from the CC-314 contract and the October audit, and the app moved onto them outside the header (CC-324), the match stage and `App.jsx` (CC-322). `Button` gains a danger look, sizes sm to lg, a link form (`as="link"`) and icon-only buttons with a required name. One `Card` (surface, raised, accent, notice; media to the edges, header, body, footer; a hover lift in CSS) replaces 42 hand-built card rules in 30 modules and the 6 px strip copies. `PageHeader` and titled `Section`s give every page one title style and one rhythm, with the owner's rules for dense pages (a heading and one line per section, colour only with meaning and a word, units and time windows, aligned numbers). `EmptyState`, `ErrorState` and `Skeleton` replace 22 bare loading lines and the dead ends (404, missing profile, missing or taken-back challenge, invalid friend link, closed or missing room), and a visitor's `/friends` no longer loads for ever. `Field` gains select, textarea (with a character count), date and copy kinds, used for the challenge and invite links, Year of birth and every console control. One `Tabs` replaces the console's and the leaderboard's. Tokens add weights, page and section title roles, tap targets and four breakpoints; `--text-base` and `--space-5` are replaced; the token check now refuses an unknown `var()` and any other media query width, and a test refuses a class missing from its CSS module (three were). Destructive confirms (resign, leave, block, remove a friend, delete) no longer close on a backdrop click, headings focused for screen readers draw no ring for mouse users, and footer links are at least 24 px tall. `/admin/design` shows every component in every state. No flag, no dependency, no API change.
