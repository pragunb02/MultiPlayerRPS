### 2. How others do it (sources read 2026-10-18)

- **Duolingo** ([milestone design](https://blog.duolingo.com/streak-milestone-design-animation/), 2022-01-21): the
  mascot itself is the celebration. Duo turns into a phoenix on milestones, and a share card follows that can be
  saved or posted without leaving the app. Their streak animation measurably raised day 7 retention (+1.7 %,
  [2022-01-31](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)).
- **Wordle** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle)): the share is text and coloured squares only. It
  shows how you did, never the answer or who you are, and it is what made the game spread.
- **Clash Royale and Brawl Stars** (see CC-099): profile collections are shelves of things you have, with locked
  ones shown as silhouettes and how to get them. Both moved away from timed or random boxes (Brawl Stars removed
  Boxes in December 2022, Clash Royale removed chest timers in April 2025). We keep the shelves and leave out the
  randomness.
- **Chess.com** (general knowledge, medium confidence): preset avatars and frames, and emotes in some modes with an
  easy mute. Its trophy cabinet shows awards with dates.
- **NN/g** ([empty states](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/), 2021-09-19): an empty
  state should say what is going on, teach the feature and give a direct next step. Never a blank container.
- **NN/g** ([animation duration](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/), 2020-02-09): 200 to 300 ms
  for big screen changes, and shorter for frequent ones. Page transitions are frequent, so they get the short end.
- **Game feel** (Juice it or lose it, GDC Europe 2012): small reactive details (a tilt, a mascot that reacts) make
  a place feel alive. ADR-016's amendment keeps this inside Motion and CSS, with no canvas backgrounds.
- **Dark patterns** (CCPA 2023 guidelines, see CC-099 for the fetch status, and
  [Deceptive Design](https://www.deceptive.design/types)): a sad mascot used to guilt the player is confirmshaming
  (PRODUCT_VISION §6.9 already says "never a sad Bit" for streak items). Emotes and cards are fine as long as they
  are optional and carry no hidden pressure.
- **WCAG 2.3.1, 2.3.3, prefers-reduced-motion** (see CC-099): tilts, staggers and page transitions are motion from
  interaction, so they are turned off under reduced motion.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. **Bit is the face of every feeling, kept small.** A 64 px portrait next to the result headline (CC-163's tone
   becomes his face: happy, a shrug for a quiet win, thinking with a tip after a loss, surprised on a draw), the
   empty states, the 404 and the waking-up screen. Never full-screen and never sad to make the player feel bad.
   Bit cheers only on a real win.
2. **Animated home, gently.** The hero plays its intro once, then stays still (ADR-012: nothing loops). Tiles get a
   3° tilt and glow on hover only where `(hover: hover)` matches, so phones are left out. The grid staggers in at
   40 ms a tile, 300 ms at most, on first visit only. All of it is CSS, with nothing new to download.
3. **Page transitions with the View Transitions API** through React Router's `viewTransition` (a 150 ms cross-fade
   and a 4 px rise), not Motion. That costs nothing in the bundle and does nothing where it is not supported, and
   Motion stays off pages that do not need it (owner decision 7). Rooms do not transition while a game is on.
   **Changed 2026-10-19 (owner, ADR-050 amended by ADR-052 point 7):** use React's `<ViewTransition>` around the
   route outlet if it is in the stable `react` we ship, because React Router deprecated its `viewTransition` prop
   for React 19.3 and later (reactrouter.com/how-to/view-transitions, read 2026-10-19). The router prop only if not.
4. **Trophy cabinet** on the profile: the existing grid becomes shelves by tier. Locked items are silhouettes with
   how to earn them. Newest unlocks come first with their date. There are no rarity percentages until the ADR-029
   gate opens, and no "only N players have this".
5. **Preset vector avatars and frames.** About 12 avatars drawn in the Bit style (CC-230 licence rules) and frames
   unlocked by level or achievement (CC-143). All cosmetic: never sold for money, never random (ADR-026). Minors
   get them too, because they are what a minor's share card shows (ADR-028 point 7).
6. **Emotes as CC-169 says**: six Bit emotes, one every 3 s, shown for 2 s on the sender's chip, not stored, a mute
   switch, and off for minors and guests. Delivered after CC-152 (age signal) and CC-044 (block and mute).
7. **Win cards**: a share button on the summary (the last CC-099 beat, never automatic). The card is drawn in the
   browser as an SVG turned into a PNG through a canvas (no fonts or images from outside, so the CSP is unchanged)
   and shared through the Web Share API, with "Copy text" where file sharing is not available. It shows the game,
   the result and series ("Beat Rocky 2–1 at Rock Paper Scissors"), Bit and the site address. A minor's or guest's
   card says "a ClassiCade player" and shows the avatar (ADR-028). Text share in the Wordle style comes with the
   daily puzzle (CC-144). Server-made link previews stay with CC-217.
8. **Low-end Android.** SVG and CSS only, `transform` and `opacity`, no canvas animations, no video (ADR-016 and
   IDEAS.md), images drawn as SVG components, the card PNG made only when the player presses Share, and a bundle
   budget per part (below).

## Why

The site works but feels plain next to the games players already know. A mascot, a living home page and things to
collect give it warmth and a reason to come back, as long as none of it pushes.

## Scope

- In: the eight parts above, each its own PR.
- Out: story mode characters (CC-194), new unlock rules (CC-143), coins (CC-142), seasonal themes (CC-205), video
  (IDEAS.md), WhatsApp preview images (CC-217).

Licence rules for CC-230 (proposed):

1. Bit and anything drawn for us: a written assignment of copyright (or an exclusive, perpetual, worldwide licence
   that allows changes) from the artist, kept outside the repo, with a line in the credits file.
2. Third-party art only under CC0, CC BY 4.0 (with credit) or MIT/ISC. Never NC, ND, "free for personal use",
   unknown, or anything taken from a game or another site.
3. AI-assisted drafts (not for Bit): the credits line names the tool, the date and the person who cleaned it up, and
   the tool's terms must allow commercial use.
4. A credits file (proposed `docs/CREDITS.md`) with asset, author, licence, source, date and AI-assisted yes or no.
   A test fails if an SVG component under `art/characters/` or `art/avatars/` has no credits entry.
5. Sounds stay synthesised (ADR-018), so they need no licence. Fonts stay system fonts (ADR-012).

## Owner decisions for CC-099, CC-163 and CC-153

1. **Tie rewards to the match on the server.** Add `matchId` to `achievements:unlocked` and quest
   `progress:updated` (a small server PR), or let the client use a 6 s window after the result. Recommended: the
   server.
2. **Beat timings and the 6 s cap**, played by themselves with tap to skip (recommended), or one tap per beat like
   Duolingo's Continue.
3. **Reduced motion shows the summary at once** (recommended), or keeps 200 ms opacity fades between beats.
4. **No reward toasts on the room page while the result is shown** (recommended). Rewards then live only in the
   card there.
5. **XP has no sound** and only the result, level up and one chime play (recommended).
6. **A win by forfeit, idle or claim is a quiet win**, with no confetti and a softer sound (recommended), or the
   same as any win.
7. **Page transitions with the View Transitions API** (no dependency, a note added to ADR-016), or Motion's
   `AnimatePresence` (it would put about 18 kB gzip on every page), or none.
8. **Who draws Bit**, the budget, and the form of the rights (assignment recommended), plus the credits file and
   licence rules above for CC-230.
9. **Bit after a loss**: "thinking" with a tip (recommended), or no Bit after a loss. Never sad to make the player
   feel bad.
10. **Win cards made in the browser now** (recommended), or wait for CC-217's server images. Whether guests get a
    card at all (recommended: yes, with "a ClassiCade player").
11. **CC-259**: the visitor profile and empty-leaderboard copy, and whether home gets a "how to play" strip (see
    [CC-259](CC-259.md)).
12. **Order**: CC-259 and CC-163 before the CC-157 shell, with CC-099 rendering into today's outcome section if
    CC-157 is not ready (recommended), or wait for CC-157.
