## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

The result and its rewards come from four independent pieces, and none of them knows about the others.

| Piece                                                  | What it does on a finished game                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `components/Celebrations/ResultCelebration.jsx`        | "VICTORY!" / "DEFEAT" / "DRAW" stamp (360 ms), a full-page `Confetti` portal for a win, and `XpPop` once the match's `progress:updated` arrives (kept per match by `MatchProgressProvider`, CC-235).                                  |
| `components/ProgressToasts.jsx` (mounted in `App`)     | On `progress:updated`: a toast "+30 XP", or "Level up! You reached level 2" with a full-page `LevelUpBurst` portal and the `level-up` fanfare, or the "didn't count" or computer-XP line. A guest's level-up toast carries "Save it". |
| `components/GamificationToasts.jsx` (mounted in `App`) | On `achievements:unlocked`: one toast per achievement, a second full-page `Confetti`, the `achievement` chime. On a quest `progress:updated`: "Quest complete! +40 XP" or "Daily sweep!", an `XpPop`, the same chime.                 |
| `components/SaveProgress/SaveProgressCard.jsx`         | A guest's "save your progress" card under the board after a game.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `games/roomUpdates.js` `resultSound`                   | `win`, `loss` or `draw` from the room update.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `components/Toast/ToastProvider.jsx`                   | Toasts stack at the top right (top and full width on phones), 4 s each, 8 s with an action, in one polite live region.                                                                                                                |

On the server the pushes come from the match outbox (ADR-047): record → judge → progression → gamification →
leaderboards. So the room's `finished` view comes first, then `progress:updated` (match), then
`achievements:unlocked`, `progress:updated` (quest, one per quest and the sweep), `quests:updated` and
`streaks:updated`, each a few to a few hundred milliseconds apart, and after a database blip seconds later
(retries from 2 s). Only the match progress carries a `matchId`. Achievement and quest pushes carry no match, so
the client cannot tell which game they belong to.

**What a first win looks like** (MATCH_EXPERIENCE.md A9, re-read in the code on 2026-10-18): the "VICTORY!" stamp,
confetti, the win sound, then within about a second the level-up burst and fanfare, a second confetti shower and
chime for "First win", up to five toasts (level up, one or two achievements, one or two quests) covering about 85 %
of a phone screen, the result headline and Rematch included, and the save progress card further down. Four sounds
overlap. Screen readers hear the result plus five toasts in a row. Each piece honours reduced motion on its own, so
under reduced motion the toasts still pile up.

Gaps:

1. No queue: every push celebrates the moment it arrives.
2. Two full-page effects can be on screen together (confetti twice, confetti and burst).
3. Rewards sit in toasts over the content, not in the result.
4. Up to four reward sounds in one second.
5. No skip, and nothing pauses for a rematch question.
6. Achievement and quest pushes cannot be tied to a match.

PR #67 (CC-208, open) adds `lib/notify/categories.js` with six categories: request (priority 4), turn (2), result
(3), reward (1, 1 s sound gap), social (2) and system (5). It gives every toast a category, adds a `hold(category)`
so that lower-priority toasts wait while a request banner is up, and per-category volume and on/off in `cc.sound`.
This plan builds on that and does not add a second system.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. **A result card with one beat slot.** Rewards play inside the result card (ADR-020 and MATCH_EXPERIENCE §3
   point 4), one beat at a time, from a pure queue. Toasts are for rewards that arrive when the player is
   somewhere else. That keeps the vision's rule of at most two lines above Rematch on a phone (principle 9), and
   Rematch never moves.
2. **One effect and one sound per beat**, and never two full-page effects at once. Confetti is for the result of a
   win only. A level up gets its rays inside the card, not over the page.
3. **Never blocking.** Beats play by themselves, a tap or any key skips to the summary, and Rematch, New opponent
   and Leave work from the first frame. A request from the opponent (PR #67 `hold`) pauses the queue, and an
   accepted rematch ends it.
4. **Cosmetic only, with no pressure.** No countdown, no "streak at risk", no "don't lose…", no random reveals, no
   coins. Copy states facts ("Level 3", "First win"). The save-progress beat for guests comes last, has a plain
   Close, never blocks, and shows at most 3 times in total (PRODUCT_VISION §6.9).
5. **Minors (ADR-028).** The beats are the same for everyone, because they are cosmetic and contain no social
   pressure. Nothing in the queue asks for push permission or shows a username to others, and no share prompt
   carries a minor's or guest's name (CC-153).
6. **Low-end Android.** Only `transform` and `opacity` animate (ADR-012), with no canvas and no new dependency. One
   effect is mounted at a time, nothing loops, and the queue is a few hundred lines of plain JS (estimate under
   3 kB gzip). It loads with the room page, never before the first move.
7. **Screen readers** hear the result at once (already urgent in the room's status), then one polite summary when
   the queue ends ("+30 XP. Level 3. Achievement unlocked: First win. Quest complete, +40 XP"), not five
   announcements.

## Why

A first win, the moment that should make a new player stay, is today the noisiest and least readable screen we
have. Rewards bury the result and the Rematch button (A9).

## Scope

- In: a pure reward queue, a result-card beat slot, a single celebration stage (at most one effect), routing of the
  existing XP, level, achievement, quest, streak and save-progress feedback through it on the room page, the skip,
  the pause for requests, the summary announcement, the reduced-motion order, and tests.
- Out: new rewards (CC-143, CC-145), outcome words and sounds (CC-163), Bit (CC-153), share cards (CC-153, CC-217),
  coins (CC-142).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On a first win, at most one celebration effect and one reward sound are active at any moment (browser test).
- [ ] Rewards appear in the documented order with the documented timings (unit tests with fake timers).
- [ ] A tap or key skips to the summary, and Rematch is usable and does not move during the whole sequence.
- [ ] An incoming request pauses the queue, and an accepted rematch ends it.
- [ ] No reward toast while the result card is on screen. Rewards arriving elsewhere still toast.
- [ ] Reduced motion: same order, no `[data-effect]` mounted, all lines readable.
- [ ] One polite summary announcement.
- [ ] Tests cover the change, and `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check` and `npm test` pass.
- [ ] Docs updated (BACKLOG status, KNOWN_ISSUES, PROGRESS).

## Plan / notes

### Sequence and timings

The clock starts when the finished room view arrives (t = 0). A beat is shown only if it applies. A beat waits for
its data, but at most 6 s (an outbox retry). After that, a late reward goes to the summary list, or to a toast if
the player has left the result.

| #   | Beat                           | Starts                           | Lasts             | Effect (one at a time)                                                 | Sound (PR #67 category)                                                 | Words (always on screen)                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Result (CC-163 moment)         | 0                                | 1.2 s             | Stamp (360 ms). Win: confetti once (1.2 s). Loss: soft dim. Draw: none | `win` / `loss` / `draw` and CC-163's reason sounds (result)             | Headline and reason (CC-162, CC-163)                                      |
| 2   | Series change                  | 1.2 s                            | 0.3 s             | The match bar dot fills                                                | none                                                                    | "You 2 – 1 Bob"                                                           |
| 3   | XP                             | 1.5 s (or when the update comes) | 0.6 s             | The XP bar fills in the card, `XpPop` "+30 XP"                         | none (XP is every game, so it stays silent)                             | "+30 XP", or why it didn't count (CC-095), or the computer-XP line        |
| 4   | Level up                       | after XP                         | 1.2 s             | Rays and the badge inside the card (`LevelUpBurst` scoped to the card) | `level-up` (reward)                                                     | "Level 3"                                                                 |
| 5   | Achievements (at most 3)       | after level                      | 0.9 s each        | The medal pops into the card                                           | `achievement`, once for the group (reward, 1 s gap)                     | "First win", then "+N more" linking to the profile                        |
| 6   | Quests                         | after achievements               | 0.6 s each, max 2 | The XP bar fills again, a scroll icon                                  | none (the chime already played, or one `achievement` if no beat 4 or 5) | "Quest complete: Win 2 games, +40 XP". A sweep merges into one line       |
| 7   | Streak (first game of the day) | after quests                     | 0.6 s             | `StreakFlame` flickers 3 times                                         | none                                                                    | "3 day streak". Never "at risk"                                           |
| 8   | Save progress (guests)         | last                             | stays             | none                                                                   | none                                                                    | "Keep your level on any device" [Create account] [Close], at most 3 times |
| 9   | Summary                        | after the last beat or a skip    | stays             | none                                                                   | none                                                                    | Every line above as a short list below Rematch (folded on phones)         |

A typical first win with a level up, one achievement and one quest takes about 1.2 + 0.3 + 0.6 + 1.2 + 0.9 + 0.6 =
4.8 s. The cap is 6 s of beats, and anything beyond it goes straight to the summary. Gaps between beats are 120 ms
(`--duration-fast`). Durations come from tokens, so reduced motion sets them all to 0.

**Reduced motion** (recommended): no effects are mounted (as today). The beat slot shows the same lines in the same
order with 0 ms between them, so the player sees the summary at once. The summary announcement is unchanged.

**Skip**: a tap on the card, Enter, Space or Escape jumps to beat 9, and sounds that have not played are dropped.
Rematch, New opponent and Leave are always live and are never covered.

**Pause**: while PR #67's request category holds the screen (an incoming rematch or a draw answer), the queue
pauses and resumes 300 ms after the banner closes. Accepting a rematch ends the queue at beat 9. The next game
starts clean.

### Where each item sits

- **Desktop (1280 × 720):** the result card over the board (MATCH_EXPERIENCE layout). From top to bottom: the
  headline and reason, the beat slot (one row, 3 rem high, content swaps in place), the XP bar, then Rematch, New
  opponent, Leave, then the summary list. The confetti is the only page-level effect, portalled, with
  `pointer-events: none`. Everything else stays inside the card.
- **Mobile (390 × 844):** the bottom sheet. The headline and the beat slot are the two lines above Rematch
  (principle 9). The summary list is folded under "Rewards (4)" below the buttons. The toast region is unchanged
  for the rest of the site.
- **Toasts:** while a result card is mounted, reward-category toasts are routed into the queue instead of the
  toast region. Elsewhere (a quest XP that arrives after the player went home) they toast as today.

### How it is built

1. `apps/web/src/lib/rewards/rewardBeats.js` (pure): `beatsFor({ outcome, match, unlocks, quests, streak, guest })`
   gives the ordered beats with `kind`, `text`, `effect`, `sound` and `ms`, applying the caps and merges.
   `advance(state, event)` is the reducer: data arrived, timer elapsed, skip, hold, release, rematch started.
   There is no I/O and no timers inside.
2. `useRewardQueue(room)`: collects the pushes of the room's current match (by `matchId`, see owner decision 1),
   drives the reducer with `setTimeout`, plays sounds through the injected player, and announces the summary.
3. `CelebrationStage`: the single portal that mounts at most one effect (`data-effect`). `ResultCelebration`,
   `ProgressToasts` and `GamificationToasts` stop mounting effects of their own on the room page.
4. **Server (small, its own PR if the owner agrees):** `achievements:unlocked` and quest `progress:updated` gain the
   `matchId` that caused them. Without it, the client falls back to "pushes within 6 s after this room finished".
5. `SaveProgressCard` becomes beat 8. Its rules (CC-097, at most 3 times) move with it.

### Sound mapping (on PR #67)

| Sound                                    | Category | When in the queue                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `win`, `loss`, `draw`, CC-163's new ones | result   | Beat 1 only                                                                           |
| `level-up`                               | reward   | Beat 4                                                                                |
| `achievement`                            | reward   | Once for beats 5 and 6 together, and only if no level up played in the last second    |
| XP, streak, save progress, summary       | none     | Silent                                                                                |
| `notify` (a request)                     | request  | Pre-empts: the queue pauses, and reward sounds that were due are dropped, not delayed |

At most three sounds per finished game: the result, a level up and one chime. The reward category's 1 s gap in #67
already enforces the spacing. The queue drops instead of queuing, so sounds never trail behind the picture.

### Tests

1. Unit (`rewardBeats.test.js`): the order for every combination (win, loss, draw, didn't count, computer, level
   up, 0 to 5 achievements, 0 to 3 quests, sweep, streak, guest or account); caps and "+N more"; the 6 s cap;
   timings summed against the table.
2. Unit with fake timers (`useRewardQueue.test.jsx`, `vi.useFakeTimers()` and `advanceTimersByTime`): beats advance
   on time; late data waits at most 6 s; skip jumps to the summary and drops sounds; hold pauses and release
   resumes; rematch ends; reduced motion shows everything at t = 0; `fakeSound()` records at most one sound per
   beat and at most three in total; one summary announcement.
3. Component: no reward toast while the card is mounted, and the toast comes back after it unmounts.
4. Browser (`e2e/celebrations.spec.js`, desktop and mobile): a new guest's first win against a computer character.
   A probe installed before the game ends (MutationObserver with timestamps) records every `[data-effect]` and
   `[data-beat]` mount and unmount. It asserts that at most one effect is mounted at any time, that the beats come
   in order, that the Rematch button's bounding box never changes and stays clickable, that the toast region holds
   no reward toast, and that at 390 × 844 at most two lines sit above Rematch. A reduced-motion run
   (`page.emulateMedia({ reducedMotion: "reduce" })`) finds no `[data-effect]` and every line present.
5. Visual baselines (CC-155) for the summary state of each outcome.
