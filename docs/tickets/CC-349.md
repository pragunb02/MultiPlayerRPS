## Problem

A first-time player cannot learn how to play where they are: the lobby's three steps are at the very bottom
(several screens down on a phone, CL-15), a friend who arrives by an invite or a challenge link never sees them
(CL-23), and nothing in a game, chess included, shows the rules (CL-24). Root cause: the steps (CC-259) were drawn
by the lobby page only, as its last section. The lobby's other words have small gaps too (CL-16 to CL-22).

## Fix

Behind the flag `how_to_play`: the lobby shows "How to play: 3 steps" as one line under the title, which opens in
place (a native `details`), the invite chooser and a received challenge show the same line under their question,
and every match (online, against the computer and pass and play) has a "How to play" button in the match bar that
opens the app's one `Modal` titled "How to play {game}" with the steps and "Back to the game". With the flag off the
lobby is as before. The lobby's copy fixes (CL-16 to CL-22) ship without a flag, as fixes of existing words.

**Research.** Today `pages/PlayPage/HowToPlay.jsx` reads `gameView(gameId).howToPlay` and the lobby draws it after
four panels. Apple's HIG (Onboarding) says a tutorial should stay "easy for people to find if they want to view it
later", and Nielsen's heuristic 10 asks for help that is easy to find and focused on the task (both cited in
CLARITY-AUDIT.md, checklist C1). For ClassiCade: the same three short steps (CC-259 already wrote them, the audit found them clear), shown where the
player is, never covering the board and never pausing the game. The dialog is the existing `Modal`
(CONTRACT §5, one dialog at a time), so Escape, a tap outside and the return of focus are already tested.

**Decisions (coordinator, design lead, 2026-10-22).** One to three steps per game, each under about 90 characters,
checked by A2. The steps are English only: no lobby, invite or match instruction has a Hinglish twin today, and C9
asks for "tum" only where Hinglish exists. The steps live in `games/howToPlay.js`, which only the lazy pages import,
so they download with the lobby, room, challenge and pass and play chunks and never with the first route.

**Cut (appetite).** CL-17's new button name "Invite a friend" for "Create room" is not in this pull request: 38
browser and unit tests in 27 files read "Create room", and other builders own most of those files. Its new line
above the button ships. The rename is left for the coordinator to ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given every game in the client registry, when A2 runs, then it has 1 to 3 How to play steps, each under
  90 characters.
- **AC-2** Given the flag on and a 390 × 844 phone, when a visitor opens a lobby, then "How to play: 3 steps" is
  above the fold, closed, and opens in place to the steps.
- **AC-3** Given the flag on, when a friend opens an invite link or a challenge link, then the same steps are there
  before they choose how to join.
- **AC-4** Given the flag on, in any match (online, computer, pass and play), when the player presses "How to play",
  then a dialog titled "How to play {game}" shows the steps, the game goes on, Escape or a tap outside closes it,
  focus comes back to the button, and the board does not move.
- **AC-5** Given the flag off, then the lobby shows the steps at the bottom as before, and neither the invite nor
  the match shows them.

## Rollout

Flag `how_to_play` (CC-312): audience everyone, client, off in code, on in every test (the unit fakes answer it on
and the e2e server seeds an override). Ramp: admins, 5 %, 25 %, 50 %, on, a day or more apart. Rollback: Off in the
console. Remove by 2027-01-15.
