## Why

Logs live only in Render (7 to 14 days, hard to search) and there are no metrics dashboards or alerts beyond the
uptime check. The owner asked (2026-10-20) whether Grafana could carry metrics and logs.

## Research (written before any code)

To be written by the builder: OpenTelemetry for Node (SDK, auto-instrumentation for HTTP, Express and Socket.IO,
OTLP export, the Collector or direct export), Grafana Cloud's free tier limits and data residency, self-hosted
Grafana OSS (Grafana, Prometheus or Mimir, Loki, Tempo) and what it would cost to host, and how other small
products scrub personal data from telemetry. Sources and dates.

## Scope

- In: an ADR (operations telemetry may go to a third party; product analytics stay first-party per ADR-034), the
  SDK wired in the composition root, a scrubber so no id, email, IP, cookie or session leaves (with a test over
  every exported attribute), dashboards for latency p50 and p95 per socket event and route, error rate, memory and
  event-loop lag, alerts to the owner, DATA.md and privacy page updates.
- Out: product analytics (CC-173 to CC-178), paid plans.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] ADR accepted by the owner before any data leaves
- [ ] No personal data in any exported metric, log or trace, proved by a test
- [ ] Dashboards and two alerts working, with evidence screenshots in the PR
- [ ] Turning it off is one env var; the app runs the same without it
