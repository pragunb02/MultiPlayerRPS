## Research (written before any code)

Sources were read on 2026-10-19 unless another date is given. "General knowledge" carries a confidence level.

### 1. How our code does it today

1. The profile has an account section and "Your data and privacy" (`PrivacySettings.jsx`: export, deletion and the
   birth year, CC-035 and CC-152). It has no social switches, because no social feature exists yet.
2. The age group is worked out from the birth year (`packages/shared/src/ages.js`, ADR-048.8). Unknown age is a
   minor.
3. PRODUCT_VISION.md §5.12 draws a "Privacy and safety" panel (who can add me, emotes, profile public or friends
   only, blocked players, report history) and the seven safe defaults for minors.

### 2. How others do it

1. **Lichess** keeps who may reach you as preferences (`modules/pref/src/main/Pref.scala`,
   https://github.com/lichess-org/lila): challenges ("Never", "If rating is ± 300", "Only friends", "If
   registered", "Always"), messages ("Only existing conversations", "Only friends", "Always"), and follow on or
   off. Kid mode removes the chat entirely (`ui/lib/src/chat/chatCtrl.ts`).
2. **UK Children's Code**, standard 7 (default settings: high privacy by default) and standard 15 (online tools,
   prominent and easy for a child), cited in PRODUCT_VISION.md §5.12 and CC-044.
3. **Discord** turns on safety alerts and media filters for teens by default (general knowledge, medium confidence).

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. One "Privacy and safety" section on the profile, the home of every social switch as its feature ships:
   1. Who can challenge me: people I played with by link, friends, nobody (CC-139, CC-277).
   2. Who can add me as a friend: people I played with, nobody (CC-052).
   3. Profile: friends only or public, for adults (CC-034).
   4. Emotes in matches: on or off (CC-169).
   5. Blocked players, with unblock, and the status of my reports (CC-044).
2. Only the switches of features that exist are shown. No switch for a feature that is not built.
3. For a minor or unknown age, the switches are shown **locked** at the safe defaults with a plain "Why?" that
   explains the rule. No hint to change the birth year (CC-152 rule, ADR-048).
4. Stored on the account in one small document (`player_privacy`), with `transferPlayer`, `erasePlayer` and
   `exportPlayer` participants and a DATA.md entry. Guests use the defaults and store nothing.

## Why

Every social ticket needs a place for its switches, and the minors' defaults must be visible and explained, not
hidden.

## Scope

- In: the section, the stored choices, the locked defaults with "Why?", the participants, DATA.md and the privacy
  page.
- Out: notification switches (CC-215), each feature's own enforcement (in its ticket).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An adult can change each shown switch, and the server enforces it.
- [ ] A minor or unknown age sees the switches locked at the defaults with "Why?", and the server refuses a change.
- [ ] Guests store nothing.
- [ ] Deletion, export and the guest transfer cover the new store.
- [ ] Tests cover the change. `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm test` pass.
- [ ] Docs updated: DATA.md, privacy page, BACKLOG, PROGRESS.
