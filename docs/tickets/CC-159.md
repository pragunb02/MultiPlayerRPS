## Why

A rematch request lived in `room.rematch.requestedBy` and a draw offer in the chess engine's `drawOfferBy`. Neither
expired, neither had a cooldown, a player could offer a draw on every move, and every kind of request needed its own
events, its own decline and its own "how did it end" field. The game shell (ADR-020) wants one request feature every
game plugs into.

## Scope

- In: `room.requests` with `type`, `from`, `to`, `createdAt`, `expiresAt`, `state` (open, accepted, declined,
  withdrawn, expired) and `endedAt`. Rematch (60 s) and the chess draw offer (30 s) moved onto it. Expiry through the
  existing every second deadline sweep (`dueAt`). One open request per type, a 10 s re-ask cooldown, at most 3 draw
  offers per player per game, the last answer kept 10 s for both sides. Two generic socket events replace the old
  rematch events and chess draw actions.
- Out: requests between more than two players (refused with a clear error, no engine needs them), challenges from
  outside a room (CC-139).

## Design

- `domain/rooms/roomRequests.js` holds the pure rules: `ruleFor` (rematch from `roomLifetimes`, game kinds from the
  engine's `requestTypes`), `settleRequests` (expire, drop requests that no longer make sense, forget old answers),
  `addRequest` (all refusals as typed errors), `endRequest`, `declineByMoving`, `requestsLeft`, `askAgainAt`.
- The room component settles the requests of every room it saves, so no request outlives its time or its game, and
  publishes `room.requestsChanged` when they change (CC-207 pushes it to both players).
- The engine contract gained optional `requestTypes` with `acceptRequest`. Chess declares `draw` (30 s, 3 per player
  per game) and accepting it ends the game by agreement. An accepted game request is logged as `agree`, so the match
  replays. The chess actions `offer-draw`, `accept-draw`, `decline-draw` and `withdraw-draw` and the view fields
  `drawOfferBy` and `drawAnswer` are gone. `CHESS_DRAW_ANSWERS` is gone from `@classicade/shared`.
- Asking for what the other player already asked you accepts it, so both pressing Rematch still starts the game, and
  both offering a draw is agreeing. Making a move declines what the opponent asked during the game (ADR-019 point 3).
- Socket API (`SOCKET_EVENTS`): `room:request {code, type}` and `room:answer {code, type, answer}` with
  `answer` one of `accept`, `decline`, `withdraw`. `room:rematch` and `room:rematch-decline` are removed.
- RoomView: `requests` (open ones and ended ones for 10 s), `requestsLeft` and `askAgainAt` for the viewer,
  `rematch` keeps `available`, `until` and `leftName` only.

"At most 3 draw offers per game" is counted per player: one player offering three times should not use up the
other's offers. ADR-019 still holds (requests come from the RoomView), but its point 1 now reads "the server's
`requests` in the RoomView, worded by the client registry's `requestTexts`" instead of "computed from game fields".
That amendment is for the owner to record in DECISIONS.md.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Requests with type, from, to, times and state, open and recently ended, in the stored room and the view.
- [x] Rematch and chess draw offers run on it. Expiry at 60 s and 30 s through the deadline sweep.
- [x] One open request per type, 10 s cooldown, 3 draw offers per player per game, answers kept 10 s.
- [x] CC-101 behaviour kept: the rematch window still closes the room after 30 minutes and answers GONE.
- [x] Unit tests (`roomComponent.rematch.test.js`, `roomComponent.requests.test.js`, the engine contract kit),
      socket tests (`rematch.test.js`, `chess.test.js`), MongoDB integration (`requests.integration.test.js`).

## Deployment impact

Rooms stored before this change keep working: their old `rematch.requestedBy` and `declinedBy` are ignored, so an
open rematch request at the moment of the deploy is dropped and the player simply asks again. A chess draw offer
standing at the deploy is dropped the same way. Tabs open from before the deploy send the old events until they load
the new version, and those answer with an error under the button.
