## Why

Wins, XP, quest progress, achievements and leaderboard places were all paid per recorded match, with no check on
how the match went. A script (or two friends in two tabs) could create a room, join it and leave at once, again and
again: every instant forfeit gave the "winner" 30 XP, a win, quest progress and a place on the boards. Two guests
could farm each other the same way and then merge everything into an account on sign-up (ADR-014). The boards
must be fair before they gain any players (ROADMAP Batch 1).

## Scope

In:

1. **One place for the rules**: `apps/server/src/domain/matches/fairPlay.js` (pure functions, constants in
   `FAIR_PLAY_LIMITS`). The reasons players can be told are shared constants, `NOT_COUNTED_REASONS` in
   `packages/shared/src/fairPlay.js`.
2. **Ended too soon**: a game counts for nobody when a player never made a move, or when someone left (result reason
   `forfeit`) before every player made `MIN_MOVES_EACH = 2` moves. The match stays in history with its result.
3. **Daily limit per opponent pair**: after `GAMES_PER_OPPONENT_PER_DAY = 10` counted games between the same two
   players in one UTC day, further games still play and are recorded but count for neither of them that day.
   Guests are counted by their id like everyone else.
4. **Hourly limit per player**: at most `GAMES_PER_HOUR = 20` counted games per player per UTC clock hour.
5. **Judged once, stored with the match**: a new pipeline step `matchComponent.judge` runs right after the match is
   recorded (and after a guest's match was moved to their account, so it judges the players as they are now). It
   stores `credit: { notCounted: [{ playerId, reason }] }` on the match with a conditional write (the first verdict
   stays). Progression, gamification and leaderboards only read that verdict through `creditFor`, so every step,
   every retry and every recount gives the same answer. The room component is unchanged.
6. **What a game that doesn't count gives**: no XP, no `matchesPlayed`, no win, loss or draw counter, no quest or
   achievement progress, no streak, no leaderboard entry or weekly XP. Progression still applies it once (with a
   zero increment) so the player hears why exactly once. `countWins` (wins boards and achievements) skips it.
7. **Guest merge (ADR-014)**: when a guest's games move to an account, oldest first, each game that counted for the
   guest is judged again against the account's games of that day. If the account already reached a limit, the game
   stops counting and is marked `withdrawnFrom: <guest id>`. The progress merge then takes those games' XP and
   counters out of the guest's totals before adding them (never below zero), stats are rebuilt from counted games
   only, and the weekly board XP is summed from counted games only. So merged totals never exceed what the limits
   would have allowed had all the games been played on the account.
8. **Players see why**: `progress:updated` carries `counted` and `notCountedReason`. The web app shows a toast
   ("This game didn't count: it ended too soon", "This game didn't count: daily limit with this opponent reached",
   "This game didn't count: you reached the limit of counted games this hour") in place of the XP toast, and the
   same words stay under the result banner. Match history (`GET /api/matches`, `/api/matches/:id`) returns
   `countsForYou: { counted, reason? }` for the viewer only.
9. Matches recorded before this change have no verdict and count, as they always did.

Out:

- Idle games that never end (CC-036) and turn deadlines. Those games are not recorded today, so they give nothing.
- Bot games (CC-138, ADR-025 bot rewards) will need their own rule.
- Showing "didn't count" in the profile's match list (the API already returns it, a small follow-up).
- The opponent's side of a merge: a guest who played the same opponent both as a guest and on their account
  keeps the opponent's credit for those games (see open questions).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An instant forfeit gives no win, XP, quest, achievement or leaderboard credit to anyone, and stays in history
- [x] Daily limit per opponent pair and hourly limit per player, with every boundary tested on an injected clock
- [x] The verdict is stored once, so progression stays exactly-once and retries change nothing
- [x] A guest's merged games never exceed the limits for the account
- [x] Players are told why, in the toast and under the result
- [x] Unit tests for every branch, MongoDB integration tests, Playwright e2e

## ADR-021 (accepted 2026-10-17, recorded in DECISIONS.md) — Fair play: when a recorded game counts

**Context.** Every reward (XP, wins, quests, achievements, streaks, boards) was paid per recorded match. That makes
instant forfeits and endless short games against a friend or a second tab a free XP machine (SEC-19).

**Decision.**

1. A match is judged once for each player, right after it is recorded, and the verdict is stored on the match.
   Every reward step reads the stored verdict, never the clock or the other games again. This keeps all steps
   idempotent and consistent with each other and with recounts (gamification's stats rebuild, weekly board XP).
2. **Moves, not seconds**, decide "ended too soon": a player who never moved, or a forfeit before both players made
   two moves. Waiting costs a script nothing, so a minimum duration would only slow down honest quick games (a
   one-round Rock Paper Scissors match takes seconds and counts). Two moves each is the smallest amount of real
   play in all three games, and leaving before it is almost always an abort, not a contest.
3. **Ten counted games per opponent pair per UTC day.** Friends who play each other a lot (a long evening of Tic-Tac-Toe
   is easily more than ten games) keep playing, only the reward stops. Ten games is 300 XP at most from one
   opponent per day, about three levels at the start and much less later, so a pair cannot climb the boards
   together. UTC matches daily quests and streaks (ADR-013).
4. **Twenty counted games per player per UTC clock hour.** That is one counted game every three minutes, above what
   an honest player finishes, and caps a script that rotates many guest opponents at 480 counted games a day.
5. The limits count every other game of the day that counts for the player or is not judged yet, before and after
   this one. So two games judged at the same moment can only under-count, never over-count, and a guest's games
   moved into an account later are held to the account's whole day.
6. A guest's game that counted for the guest but would pass a limit for the account is marked `withdrawnFrom` and
   its credit is taken out of the merged totals (ADR-014 stays exactly-once: the marks are written before progress
   merges, and the merge recomputes them on resume).

**Consequences.** One extra indexed query per player per match (`players.playerId, endedAt`). Old matches count.
Limits are constants in one file, so tuning them is a one-line change with tests. Ratings (CC-042) can reuse the
verdict.

## Open questions for the coordinator

- The opponent of a merged guest is not re-judged: if Bob played 10 games with guest Riya and 10 with account Riya
  on the same day, Riya ends up with 10 counted, Bob keeps 20. Re-judging Bob would mean taking XP back from a player
  who did nothing wrong, so this stays as is unless you prefer otherwise.
- Quest bonus XP that a guest earned from games later withdrawn in a merge stays with the guest's totals (the quest
  was completed and paid on that day). It is at most one day's quest bonus.

## Verification

- Unit: `fairPlay.test.js` (every boundary: 0/1/2 moves, forfeit vs. normal end, 9/10 games per pair, 19/20 per
  hour, UTC day and hour edges, other days and the match itself ignored), plus the match, progression,
  gamification and leaderboard component tests, the shared DAO contract (in memory and MongoDB), and the pipeline
  wiring in `server.test.js`.
- Socket end to end (`progressRoutes.test.js`): an instant leave pushes `counted: false` with the reason and gives
  nobody XP.
- MongoDB integration (`server.integration.test.js`): an instant leave through the real composition root is stored
  with its verdict, gives no XP, win or achievement, and history says why. A guest who played 6 games with the same
  opponent as the account's 6 merges in only 4 counted games (10 total), and all 12 stay in history.
- Playwright (`e2e/fairPlay.spec.js`): an instant forfeit in Tic-Tac-Toe shows "This game didn't count: it ended too
  soon" in the toast and under the result, and the winner's progress stays at 0 XP.
