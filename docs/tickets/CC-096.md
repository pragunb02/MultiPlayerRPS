## Why

Sessions expired a fixed 7 days after they were created, however often the player came back (express-session only
re-sends the cookie when the session changes). A guest who played every day lost their session on day 8; their XP,
streak and achievements stayed in the database under a guest id nothing linked to any more, so they could never
graduate them into an account (CC-032).

## Scope

- `rolling: true`: every response re-issues the cookie with a fresh expiry.
- Lifetime 30 days after the last visit, for guests and accounts (`SESSION_TTL_SECONDS`).
- The store rewrites an unchanged session's expiry at most once an hour (`touchAfterSeconds`, default 3600) — a
  visit costs no database write most of the time; MongoDB's TTL index still deletes expired sessions.
- Sessions already in production roll on their next visit; no migration.
- Out: prompts that ask guests to save their progress (CC-097); cleaning up abandoned guest data (CC-098).

## Verification

- Unit (simulated clock): a guest who returns on day 25 is still signed in and the new cookie expires 30 days after
  that visit; one who returns after 31 days is not; visitors without a session still get no cookie. The first test
  fails with `rolling: false`.
- Integration on MongoDB: a touch moves the stored expiry forward; within the touch interval it doesn't write.
- 1428 tests (lines 100 %, branches 99.58 %), 116 browser tests.
