The board row says: a daily set, each puzzle with its own streak and a spoiler-free share grid. Part 1 is the chess
puzzle from the Lichess CC0 puzzle database. The Ultimate Tic Tac Toe "win in 3" and the RPS "read today's
character" are later parts, and the design below lets each plug in as one more puzzle kind.

## Research (written before any code)

Sources were read on 2026-10-20 unless another date is given. "General knowledge" carries a confidence level.

### 1. How our code does it today

1. **No puzzles exist.** Nothing on the server or the web app knows the word. `docs/GROWTH.md` waits for CC-144
   for the daily WhatsApp Channel post, and `docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md` puts it ninth in phase 2.
2. **Chess pieces we can reuse.** `packages/games/src/chess/chessEngine.js` uses chess.js as the referee (ADR-041).
   The web board `apps/web/src/games/chess/Board.jsx` is already a keyboard grid (one square in the Tab order, the
   arrow keys, Home and End, Enter or Space to pick up and drop, each square named like "e4, white pawn, legal move")
   and drops the slide under reduced motion. `useBoardInput.js` turns clicks, drags and keys into one `onMove` with
   only legal moves. Neither knows about rooms, so both can serve a puzzle as they are.
3. **Streaks today** (`domain/gamification/streaks.js`, ADR-013): a win streak and a daily play streak, counted in
   UTC days until CC-146. Same day changes nothing, the next day adds one, a gap starts again at 1, and a streak
   last extended yesterday is "at risk". They are built from matches only.
4. **XP** comes from matches and quest bonuses (`progressionComponent.awardBonus`, exactly once per key). The
   leaderboards take bonus XP only from quests (`connectLeaderboardBonuses` in `server.js`).
5. **Days.** The owner console counts India days (`utils/indiaTime.js`, ADR-034). Gamification still counts UTC
   days (ADR-013 amendment: local days arrive with CC-146).
6. **Sharing** (`apps/web/src/lib/shareText.js`, CC-217): a Hinglish line then an English line, the free line, the
   link, and a `wa.me` link. No text ever takes a name (ADR-028.7).
7. **Guests and sign-up.** Everything stored by player id is an ADR-014 participant (`playerTransferComponent`), and
   an ADR-048 participant for export and deletion (`playerDataComponent`).

### 2. How other products do it

1. **The Lichess puzzle database** (https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles, read 2026-10-20). 6,157,341 puzzles,
   updated 2026-10-05, released under **Creative Commons CC0**: anyone may copy, change and redistribute them, even
   commercially, without asking. Columns: `PuzzleId, FEN, Moves, Rating, RatingDeviation, Popularity, NbPlays,
Themes, GameUrl, OpeningTags, DailyDate`. "FEN is the position before the opponent makes their move." The first
   move is played to reach the position the player sees, and "the second move is the beginning of the solution".
   Every player move is an only move, except mate in one at the end, where any checkmate wins. Moves are in UCI.
   The file is about 270 MB compressed, so it must never be fetched by the server.
2. **Lichess puzzle play** (lichess.org/training, general knowledge, high confidence). A wrong move ends the attempt
   as failed, the solution can then be viewed, and the next puzzle is offered. A hint (the "Get a hint" button)
   highlights the piece to move. Lichess' "Daily puzzle" is one puzzle a day for everyone.
3. **Chess.com Daily Puzzle** (general knowledge, medium confidence; the scale is in
   https://www.chess.com/blog/CHESScom/chess-is-booming-and-our-servers-are-struggling, read 2026-10-20, "1 million
   people a day" from December 2022). One puzzle a day for everyone, a calendar of past days, a streak, and a hint.
4. **Wordle** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wordle, read 2026-10-20). One answer a day for everyone, six tries, a
   numbered day ("Wordle 196 4/6") and a grid of coloured squares that shows how it went and never the answer. The
   grid is what made it travel: it went viral on Twitter in late December 2021 after the share was added. Statistics
   count the current and the best streak (general knowledge, high confidence: a missed or failed day resets the
   current streak to 0).
5. **The NYT Crossword** (general knowledge, high confidence) gets harder through the week, from Monday (easiest) to
   Saturday. A known rhythm brings people back on the easy days and gives the hard days a reason to share.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. **A curated set in the repo, not in MongoDB.** About 400 puzzles in a JSON file of about 60 KB, made once by a
   script from a downloaded part of the CC0 file and checked by tests: every solution is legal, ends the puzzle, and
   the client cannot import the file. A file is reviewed in the pull request, deploys with the code and needs no
   migration or new collection. The licence is recorded beside it and in `docs/CREDITS.md`.
2. **Harder through the week.** Monday 700 to 1099, then 1100 to 1399, 1400 to 1599, 1600 to 1799, 1800 to 1999, 2000
   to 2199, and Sunday 2200 and up. 58 puzzles in each band, 406 in all, which is 58 weeks before anything repeats.
3. **Three mistakes, not one.** Our players are casual chess players. Lichess ends at one wrong move, Wordle gives
   six tries. Three wrong moves end the day's attempt, and a wrong move never moves the board on.
4. **Two optional hints:** first the idea (the main theme, such as "Look for a fork"), then the piece to move. Each
   one is a 💡 on the share card, so a hint is honest without being a penalty.
5. **The solution is checked on the server with chess.js** and never sent before the attempt ends. A correct move
   gets the reply from the server. Any checkmate is accepted, as on Lichess.
6. **Its own streak, Wordle rules on ADR-013's days.** A solved day after a solved yesterday adds one, a solved day
   after a gap starts at 1, a failed day sets it to 0, and a streak last extended yesterday is at risk. It is not
   the play streak: a puzzle is not a match and never feeds the daily play streak or the quests.
7. **No XP in part 1.** XP for a puzzle would be a second XP source outside matches and quests. A guest who solves
   and then signs in to an account that solved the same day would carry two awards for one day (the progress move
   adds totals, ADR-014.5), and the leaderboards take bonus XP only from quests. That is the double counting the
   brief forbids. The reward is the streak and the share card. XP can follow with an owner decision (question 2).
8. **India days now, local days later.** The day is `dayOf(now)` from `utils/indiaTime.js` (ADR-034). It is one
   function the puzzle component is given, so CC-146 can hand it the player's own day.
9. **A spoiler-free grid that reuses CC-217.** One square per move the player had to find: 🟩 found first time,
   🟨 found after a mistake, 🟥 where it ended, ⬛ never reached. Then 💡 per hint and 🔥 with the streak. It names
   nobody, so no guest's or minor's name can be on it (ADR-028.7).

## Why

The daily ritual is the strongest return loop we can build cheaply (PRODUCT_VISION §phase 2, GROWTH.md top 10 #9).
One puzzle for everyone gives friends something to compare in WhatsApp every day.

## Scope

- In: the chess puzzle, its curated set and licence, server checking, attempts, hints, its streak, the share grid,
  guests and accounts, the move to an account on sign-up, export and deletion, the page `/daily/chess`, a card on
  the home page.
- Out: Ultimate Tic Tac Toe and RPS puzzles (later parts), XP for puzzles (question 2), a calendar of past days,
  leaderboards (CC-291 is changing them) and the outcome screen (CC-163 is building it).

## Design

### Server

1. `packages/games/src/puzzles/chessPuzzle.js`, pure, on chess.js: `startOfChessPuzzle`, `judgeChessMove`,
   `chessHint`, `chessSolution`. No data. The catalogue never sits in a package the web app imports.
2. `apps/server/src/persistence/puzzles/chessPuzzles.json` (the set, with `LICENSE.md` beside it) and
   `persistence/puzzleCatalog.js`, which reads it once at boot.
3. `persistence/dailyPuzzleAttemptDao.js`: the new collection `daily_puzzle_attempts`, one document per player,
   kind and day. Indexes: unique `{ playerId, kind, day }`. A `version` field makes every change a compare-and-set.
   The in-memory double passes the same contract.
4. `data/dailyPuzzleAccessor.js` maps documents to frozen attempts and gives the catalogue to the domain.
5. `domain/dailyPuzzles/`: `schedule.js` (the day's puzzle: day number since Monday 2026-10-19, modulo the set),
   `puzzleStreak.js` (pure streak rules), `attempt.js` (pure: a move or a hint changes an attempt), `kinds.js` (the
   chess kind: the referee, three mistakes, two hints) and `dailyPuzzleComponent.js` (today, play, hint, the move
   on sign-up, export, erase).
6. `service/http/dailyPuzzleRoutes.js`:
   1. `GET /api/daily/:kind` for anyone, visitors included: the day, the number, the position, the rating, and for
      a player the attempt and the streak.
   2. `POST /api/daily/:kind/moves` `{ day, from, to, promotion? }` and `POST /api/daily/:kind/hints` `{ day }`:
      a session is needed (401 otherwise), unknown fields are refused, a day that is no longer today gets 409, an
      illegal move 422 and no mistake.
   3. 30 changes a minute per player, and the API's general limit per address.

### Web

1. `/daily/chess` (`paths.daily("chess")`): `pages/DailyPuzzlePage`, which picks the board from a registry of kinds
   (`games/daily/dailyKinds.js`), so a new kind adds a board and a line there.
2. `games/daily/ChessPuzzle.jsx` reuses `Board.jsx` and `useBoardInput.js`, with a polite live region that reads
   each move ("You played Nf7+. Correct. Black played Kg8").
3. Mistakes left as three marks with the number in words, the hint button through `useAction`, the result with
   the grid, the streak and the share (WhatsApp and the share sheet, as CC-217's `ShareResult`).
4. A "Daily puzzle" card on the home page.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The set: 406 puzzles in 7 rating bands, each checked by a test, the CC0 notice in the repo
- [x] Today's puzzle is the same for everyone on an India day, and the next day brings the next one
- [x] The server checks every move with chess.js. No answer carries the solution before the attempt ends
- [x] Three mistakes end the attempt. Two hints, both optional
- [x] Its own streak, rebuilt after a guest signs in, never feeding XP, quests or the play streak
- [x] A spoiler-free share grid with Hinglish and English lines, no name in it
- [x] Guests and accounts play. A guest's days move to the account on sign-up. Export and deletion include them
- [x] The board works by keyboard, each move is announced, reduced motion drops the slide
- [x] Tests cover the change. `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm test` pass
- [x] DATA.md and the privacy page list the new store

## Plan / notes

Questions for the owner:

1. **The Hinglish lines** of the share card are ours, not the owner's drafts (marked in `shareText.js`).
2. **XP for a solved puzzle** (for example 20 XP, unranked, once a day). Part 1 gives none, for the reason in
   research 3.7. Doing it needs the progress move to drop a guest's puzzle award for a day the account already had.
3. **The first number.** Puzzle #1 is Monday 2026-10-19, so the number on launch day is the days since then. Change
   `FIRST_DAY` in `schedule.js` before the merge if the owner wants #1 on launch day (it must stay a Monday).
4. **Known limit.** A player can fail on a second guest to see the solution, then solve on their account. It only
   touches their own streak, which nobody else sees. A guest who signs in brings a failed day with them, so the same
   browser cannot do it.
