## Why

ClassiCade stores data about people (sessions, accounts with email addresses, match history, progress) and is
about to be shared publicly. Players need to be told, in plain words, what is kept, why and for how long, and what
the rules of the site are.

> **DRAFTS.** Both texts were written by reading the code as it is on 2026-10-16. They are **not legal advice**.
> The owner must have them reviewed by a lawyer before launch. Each page shows a visible "Draft" note until then.

## Scope

- In: `/privacy` and `/terms` pages in the web app (`paths.privacy()`, `paths.terms()`, `ROUTES.PRIVACY/TERMS`),
  a new `SiteFooter` with links to both and a one line cookie notice, replacing the old footer in `Layout`
  (the header is untouched). Head tags (title, description, canonical) come from CC-047.
- Out: a self-service "delete my account" or "download my data" button (the policy says to ask the owner until it
  exists, follow-up ticket proposed below), a contact address (owner action), translations.

## ADR-0xx (proposed): the cookie notice is a line in the footer, not a banner

- **Context.** The site sets exactly one cookie, only after the player chooses to play, and only to keep them signed
  in. That is a strictly necessary cookie, for which the ePrivacy rules and similar laws require information but
  not consent. The browser tests (`e2e/fixtures.js`) fail on anything in web storage.
- **Decision.** No consent wall and no dismissible banner. Every page's footer says "One cookie keeps you signed in.
  No ads, no trackers." with a link to `/privacy`, and the policy explains the cookie in section 3.
- **Why.** A banner that can be closed must remember that it was closed, which needs a second cookie or web
  storage, exactly what the notice promises we do not use. A consent wall for a necessary cookie would ask a
  question with only one possible answer, and would get in the way of an invite link opening straight into a game.
- **Revisit when** anything non-essential is added (analytics, ads, embeds): that needs real consent, before the
  thing loads.
