## Why

Every feature chose its own sound and its own toast, so a request could be buried under rewards, and a player could
only turn all sound on or off.

## Research

**How our code did it before.** Every feature picked its own sound and its own toast. The sound settings were one
mute switch and one volume in `cc.sound` (ADR-018), and a reward toast could cover a rematch question.

**How other products do it.**

- Discord and Slack let the user choose notification sounds per kind of event, and keep the text of a notice even
  when its sound is off ([Discord notification settings](https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/215253258)).
- Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and Android's notification channels group notices into categories, each with its
  own importance, sound and on or off switch, chosen by the user
  ([Android notification channels](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/channels),
  [Apple HIG notifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/notifications)).
- Games such as Lichess separate move sounds from social sounds and let each be turned off.

**What is best for ClassiCade.** One module with six categories (request, turn, result, reward, social, system), each
with a sound, a place on screen, a priority and a rate limit, so a request always pre-empts a reward. Per category on
or off and volume in a small settings panel, kept in the existing allowlisted `cc.sound` key (extended, old values
still read). Text and the screen reader announcement always stay, as on Android and Discord.

## Scope

- In: `lib/notify/categories.js` with six categories: request, turn (moves and your turn), result, reward, social
  (other players coming and going) and system (connection). Each has its own sound (new: `presence` and
  `connection`), its place on screen, a priority and a rate limit. Every sound belongs to a category
  (`SOUND_CATEGORIES`), except the click of a button.
  1. The sound player plays a sound at its category's volume, not at all when the category is off, and at most once
     within the category's rate limit (several in one call still play together, such as a move and the game start).
  2. Toasts carry a category. The same message is not shown twice within the rate limit. While something of a
     higher priority holds the screen (`useHold`, used by the request banner and the site popup), lower toasts wait
     and show afterwards, so requests pre-empt rewards.
  3. A settings panel (the gear beside the speaker in the header) with an on or off switch and a volume for each
     category. The words and the screen reader announcement of a notice always stay, only the sound is chosen.
  4. Existing sounds moved onto it through `SOUND_CATEGORIES`, and existing toasts through their category (rewards
     for XP, levels, achievements, quests and saved progress, social for "left the room", system for the rest).
- Out: notification preferences and quiet hours (CC-215), the inbox (CC-147).

## Storage

The choices stay in the one allowlisted key `cc.sound` (ADR-018), extended to
`{"muted":false,"volume":0.6,"categories":{"request":{"on":true,"volume":1},…}}`. A value saved before has no
`categories` and reads with every category on. The browser tests' allowlist checks the new shape exactly
(`e2e/fixtures.js`). ADR-018 describes the old two-field shape, so its point 4 needs the owner to record the
extension in DECISIONS.md.
