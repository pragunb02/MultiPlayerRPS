## Why

The owner's report: two players finish a game. Alice goes to the home page (or presses Back, or the logo) without
pressing Leave. Bob asks for a rematch. Alice sees nothing, because the question only existed on the room page, and
Bob waits for ever, because Alice still showed as connected.

## Scope

- In:
  1. A third presence, "away from the game screen": the player is on the site but not on the room's page. Leaving
     the room page sends `room:away`, and the opponent sees it at once.
  2. Requests addressed to a player reach every tab of theirs on any page as a popup ("Bob wants a rematch at Chess"
     with Accept and go and Decline), with the request sound, the tab title alert and an urgent announcement. It
     uses the existing per-player socket channel (`player:<id>`), event `request:updated`. Accept and go accepts and
     opens the room.
  3. The asker is told at once when the opponent is offline or on another page. Offline comes with Copy invite link
     (the room's link, to bring them back), New opponent and Play vs computer (the game's lobby, where the bots PR
     adds the characters).
  4. An expired request shows "No answer from Bob" with the same choices, until the asker closes it.
  5. A finished room that a player stays away from for 2 minutes lets go of their seat, and the opponent sees "Alice
     left, so there is no rematch this time."
  6. The asker on another page hears how their request ended, in a toast with the way back to the room.
- Out: an inbox of past requests (CC-147), push notifications (CC-148), challenges outside rooms (CC-139).

## Design

- Server: `roomComponent.playerAway`, `playerOnline` (offline to away for every room on connecting),
  `playerOffline` (away to offline when the last tab closes), `roomsWithOpenRequests` and `requestsView`.
  `roomHandlers.js` adds `room:away`, `handleSocketOnline` (presence and the open requests for the new socket) and
  `pushRequests`, and `handleSocketGone` now tells away from offline. The gateway changes are three lines: call
  `handleSocketOnline`, push on `room.requestsChanged`, and the restart notice (CC-158).
- Seats keep `connected` (the room page is open) and add `away` and `since`. Older seats read as before.
- Web: `useRoom` sends `room:away` when the room page goes away with a seat still held. `hooks/useSiteRequests.js`
  keeps the last push per room. `components/SiteRequests` (inside `main`, above every page) shows the popups for
  rooms other than the one on screen and the answer toasts.
