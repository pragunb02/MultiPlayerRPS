## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

- `components/Celebrations/outcome.js` reduces every ending to `win`, `loss`, `draw` or `watched`. PR #67 maps
  `aborted` to `draw`.
- `ResultCelebration` stamps one of four words ("Victory!", "Defeat", "Draw", "Game over"), with confetti for any
  win. A win because the opponent left gets the same confetti as a checkmate.
- `games/outcomeText.js` `resultHeadline` (CC-162) adds the reason: chess (checkmate, stalemate, insufficient
  material, threefold, fifty-move, resignation, agreement, on time, on time with too little material), Tic-Tac-Toe
  (three in a row, full board), RPS ("the best of 3"), and the room's forfeit. PR #67 adds `idle` ("on the move
  timer") and `aborted` ("Game aborted: the first move did not come in time") from CC-036.
- `games/roomUpdates.js` `resultSound` plays only `win`, `loss` or `draw`. A forfeit win sounds like a checkmate,
  and a timeout loss like any loss.
- The loss styling is muted text (`--color-text-muted`), with no red and no shake. That is already right.
- A room closed because everyone was away (`ROOM_CLOSE_REASONS.ABANDONED`) is a room-closed message, not a game
  result. The player who comes back sees the GONE page.

Gaps: every reason looks and sounds alike apart from one phrase. There are no kind words after a loss. A win by
forfeit or idle is celebrated like a real win. There is no moment for an aborted game. The game registry has no
`outcomeText` or `moments` yet (MATCH_EXPERIENCE §3 lists them as part of the game module contract).

### 2. How others do it (sources read 2026-10-18)

- **Chess.com** ([Game Review help](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8584089-what-is-game-review)): the
  game-over pop-up names the result and the reason ("won by resignation", "on time", "by abandonment") and offers
  Game Review with a one-line coach summary that also points to good moves in a loss. There is no confetti. A win by
  abandonment is shown plainly (general knowledge from playing, medium confidence).
- **Lichess** (general knowledge, medium confidence): words only, such as "White resigned", "Black left the game"
  or "Time out", and the reason is always shown.
- **Duolingo** (sources in CC-099): a missed or failed lesson gets an encouraging line, never a scolding.
- **Game feel** (Juice it or lose it, GDC Europe 2012, see CC-099): juice belongs to the player's own action. A win
  that came from the opponent leaving is not the player's action, so it gets less juice.
- **NN/g** ([Microinteractions](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/microinteractions/), 2018-10-21): celebrations
  should serve a purpose and fit the tone.
- **Deceptive Design** ([types](https://www.deceptive.design/types)) and the CCPA 2023 guidelines (see CC-099):
  confirmshaming means words that make the player feel bad for a choice. Loss words must never shame ("You gave
  up", "Too slow!").
- **WCAG 2.3.1** and **prefers-reduced-motion** (see CC-099): the dim after a loss is one fade, never a flash, and
  under reduced motion it is a static change.
- **Sound** (WCAG 1.4.2, see CC-099): every outcome sound stays under 1 s. A loss is a soft fall, never a buzzer.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. **One pure function, `outcomeMoment(room)`**, gives `{ tone, headline, reason, line, sound, effect, icon }` for
   every ending and viewer (player who won, lost or drew, spectator). It builds on `resultHeadline`, with each game
   adding its reasons through the registry (`outcomeText`), as ADR-020 plans.
2. **Four tones.** `win` (gold, confetti for a played-out win). `quiet-win` (gold text, no confetti, a two-note
   rise, for a forfeit, idle or claimed win, because the player did not earn it on the board). `gentle-loss`
   (neutral ink, a soft dim of the board, a low two-note fall, and kind words). `even` (draw and aborted: primary
   ink, no effect, the draw sound, or none when aborted).
3. **Kind words, never blame.** A loss line is about the game, not the player: "Good game. Rematch?", "Rocky read
   that one. Try mixing it up." With a character (ADR-025), the hint comes from the character's habit, as in story
   mode (STORY_MODE §4, "a loss teaches something").
4. **Cosmetic only.** Words and sound do not change what counts. ADR-021 and CC-095 decide that, and this ticket
   only says it.
5. **For minors.** The same words, no names of other children beyond the display name already in the room, and
   nothing that compares the player to others.
6. **Low-end phones.** Reuse the existing confetti and stamp. The dim is one opacity transition on an overlay. Two
   new sound recipes, no files (ADR-018).

## Design decisions (2026-10-20, written before the code)

These settle what the research left open. None of them needs a new ADR: they apply ADR-050 point 2, ADR-018 and
ADR-051's Hinglish answer, and ADR-052's build order (step 2).

1. **One table, one function.** `apps/web/src/games/outcomeMoment.js` holds the only map from an ending to its
   tone, stamp, effect, icon and sound (`ENDINGS`), and the room's own reasons (forfeit, idle, aborted) with their
   words. `outcomeMoment(room)` returns a frozen `{ ending, tone, stamp, headline, line, effect, icon, sound }`.
   The result card, the board dim, the screen reader announcement and `roomSounds` all read it, so nothing else
   decides how an ending looks or sounds.
2. **Six endings, four tones.** `won` (tone `win`), `won-quietly` (`quiet-win`), `lost` (`gentle-loss`), `drew` and
   `aborted` (`even`), and `watched` (`even`, a spectator of a win). A spectator of a draw or an aborted game sees
   the same as the players. Spectators never hear a result sound, as today.
3. **Games plug in through the registry.** A new optional `outcomeText` field on `GAME_VIEWS`:
   `words(reason)` gives the lines for one of the game's reasons (`won`, `lost`, `drew`, `watched`, each a
   function of the names), `quiet: true` when a win by that reason is not earned on the board, and `lostPhrase`
   when "You lost" needs other words than "You won" (Tic-Tac-Toe's "You lost to three in a row"). `hint(bot)` gives
   a computer character's tip after a loss. A game with no `outcomeText`, or a reason it does not know, gets the
   plain lines, so a new game or reason never breaks the card.
4. **Hinglish and English, as for CC-217.** Every line is a pair, the Hinglish line first and the English line
   under it (ADR-051, answered 2026-10-20). The headline stays English only: it is CC-162's result sentence, the
   page title of the result and what the tests read. Every Hinglish line here is **drafted by the builder** for the
   owner to change (the table under "Lines shipped"). Lines are at most 8 words in each language, and no line
   after a loss has "!". Names are only the display names already shown in the room (as CC-162 does).
5. **Stamp.** The big decorative word over the headline: "Victory!" for a win, "Victory" for a quiet win, "Draw",
   "No result" for an aborted game and "Game over" for a spectator. A loss gets **no stamp**: "Defeat" is gone, and
   the neutral headline is the only big text.
6. **Icons from existing art only.** The quiet win shows the gold trophy medal (`AchievementIcon` "trophy"). The
   handshake and dash icons of the research do not exist yet and wait for the icon set (CC-283). Draws and aborted
   games have no icon.
7. **The dim.** After a loss the board (only the board, not the save progress card or the buttons) fades once to
   85 % opacity in 360 ms (`--duration-slow`) and stays. Its resting style is the dimmed one, so under reduced
   motion it is dimmed with no transition. The loss state is already scanned by axe in light and dark, so the
   dimmed board is held to AA contrast.
   **Changed while building (2026-10-20):** the 85 % fade failed axe's AA contrast check on every game's loss
   state (the RPS seat names and result, the Tic-Tac-Toe marks, the chess board's labels), because lowering
   opacity lowers the contrast of every text on the board, and those texts are already close to 4.5:1. The board
   now softens its colours instead (`filter: saturate(0.35)`), one still change with no animation. Saturation
   keeps how light each colour is, so the contrast stays, and it reads the same under reduced motion. It is not an
   animated property, so ADR-012 rule 3 (only `transform` and `opacity` animate) still holds.
8. **Two new sounds** in the result category, so the result switch and volume of the sound settings (`cc.sound`)
   govern them like `win`: `win-quiet` (C5 then G5, sine, 0.3) and `game-void` (one A4, sine, 0.2, 0.15 s). Both
   are our own synthesised recipes in `sounds.js` (no file, no licence beyond our own, CREDITS.md already covers
   the recipes).
9. **No clash with toasts or the request banner.** While the moment plays (1.2 s, `--duration-celebrate`) the
   result card holds the result category (`useHold`), so lower toasts (rewards, other players) wait until it is
   done, one beat at a time (CC-099's beat 1). A request and a lost connection are more important and still show
   at once. The dim is on the board and the request banner sits above the card, so neither covers the other.
   **Added while building (2026-10-20):** two details the hold needed.
   1. Only a game that ends while the viewer watches holds the screen. A result that was already there when the
      page opened (a reload, a link to a finished room) is no new moment, as it already plays no sound.
   2. The level-up burst and fanfare used to start the moment the progress update came, while their toast now
      waits for the moment. A toast now has an `onShow` that runs when it really shows, and the level-up toast
      starts its burst and fanfare there, so the three stay together and come after the result. A request holding
      the screen delays them the same way.
10. **Screen readers.** The urgent region reads the headline and the line ("You lost by checkmate. Achha game tha.
    Ek aur? Good game. Want another?"), the same words as on screen. The stamp, icon and effects stay
    `aria-hidden`. Nothing in the moment can be dismissed (no overlay, the confetti takes no clicks), so it needs
    no dismiss key. Rematch and the other buttons are never covered.
11. **Aborted headline.** "Game aborted", with the reason in the line ("No first move in time. Nothing counted."),
    as the research table planned.
12. **Not in this ticket.** Bit's faces (CC-153b), the reward queue (CC-099), the winning line glow (the board's
    own), the icon set (CC-283). Room abandoned stays the GONE page message, as the research says.

## Why

A11 and A14 in MATCH_EXPERIENCE: the reason for a result is missing or below the fold, and a forfeit reads like a
real win. A loss should make a new player want a rematch, not leave.

## Scope

- In: `outcomeMoment` and its words for every reason, the tones, two new sounds (`win-quiet` and `game-void`), the
  gentle-loss dim, icons from existing art, and the registry `outcomeText` hook. It is beat 1 of the CC-099 queue.
- Out: Bit's reactions (CC-153, which adds a face to each tone), the reward beats (CC-099), and chess accuracy or
  review (not planned).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every end reason of every game, for winner, loser and spectator, has a headline, a reason line and a tone
      (table test).
- [x] A forfeit, idle or claimed win shows no confetti and plays `win-quiet`.
- [x] A loss shows no red and no shake, dims the board once (no flash), and plays the soft `loss` sound.
- [x] An aborted game plays `game-void` and says nothing counted.
- [x] Under reduced motion the words and colours are the same, and nothing moves.
- [x] Tests cover the change, and `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check` and `npm test` pass.
- [ ] Docs updated (BACKLOG status, KNOWN_ISSUES, PROGRESS). The coordinator owns BACKLOG and PROGRESS: the board
      row and the changelog line are at the end of this file. No KNOWN_ISSUES finding is linked.

### Moments (beat 1 of CC-099)

| Tone        | Visual (≤ 1.2 s, `transform` and `opacity` only)                                                 | Reduced motion                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| win         | Gold stamp (360 ms), confetti once (24 pieces, 1.2 s), the winning line or piece glows once      | Gold headline, no confetti             |
| quiet-win   | Gold stamp, no confetti, a small trophy icon                                                     | Gold headline                          |
| gentle-loss | Neutral stamp "Defeat" replaced by the headline, the board dims to 85 % opacity in 300 ms, stays | The board is dimmed with no transition |
| even        | Primary-ink stamp, a handshake icon (draw) or a dash icon (aborted)                              | Same, static                           |

No luminance flashing: the stamp and the dim change once. The twinkle in `art.module.css` runs at 0.83 Hz for 3
cycles, well under WCAG 2.3.1's 3 a second.

### Sounds (ADR-018 recipes, result category in PR #67)

- `win` (exists): four rising notes, for a win played to the end.
- `win-quiet` (new): two soft rising sine notes (C5 → G5, 0.3 gain), for forfeit, idle and claimed wins.
- `loss` (exists): a soft three-note fall. It stays as it is: no low buzz, nothing harsh.
- `draw` (exists).
- `game-void` (new): one short neutral note (A4, 0.2 gain, 0.15 s), for an aborted game.
- `sounds.test.js` keeps every note soft and short. `categories.test.js` (PR #67) gets the two new sounds under
  `result`.

### How it is built

1. `games/outcomeMoment.js` (pure) with the table above, using `resultHeadline` for the headline, the registry's
   per-game `outcomeText` for lines and hints, and `ROOM_END_REASONS` from PR #67.
2. `ResultCelebration` takes the moment (tone, effect, icon) instead of `outcomeFor`, and the reward beats move to
   CC-099's queue.
3. `resultSound` in `roomUpdates.js` returns `moment.sound`.
4. A CSS module class for the board dim on the board wrapper (no inline style, ADR-012 rule 6).
