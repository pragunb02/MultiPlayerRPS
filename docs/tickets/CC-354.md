Shaped with [CC-355](CC-355.md) and [CC-356](CC-356.md) after the owner lifted the feature freeze for these three on
2026-10-22 (ADR-082). We take the general pattern (a few small goals that refill), never another game's theme, art
or words.

## Problem

Asha opens ClassiCade on her phone in a five minute break. Her three daily quests ask for "Win 3 matches" and "Finish
5 matches": goals for a whole evening, not for five minutes. She plays one game against Rocky, nothing on screen
moves except the XP bar, and she closes the tab. Nothing tells her what a good next game would be, and nothing small
is ever finished in a short visit. Once the daily quests are done (or clearly out of reach), there is no small thing
to do at all until tomorrow.

## Job to be done

When I have a few minutes, I want a small goal I can finish in one or two games, and a new one as soon as it is done,
so every short visit ends with something finished and I know what to play next.

## Appetite

L (up to 2 days of agent time), in two PRs: (1) server: catalogue, pure rules, storage, pipeline step, routes, the
transfer, export and erase; (2) web: the card, the toast, the flag paths, the browser test. **Cut line**, dropped
first in this order: Swap goal (the goals still refill); the guest-to-account merge of a guest's completed count
(the account keeps its own goals, the guest's are deleted; XP already moves with progress); the per-goal Play
link (the card still names the game). Never cut: three goals that refill, no expiry, the daily cap, ADR-021 counting,
the transfer participant, export and erase, the flag with both paths.

## Proposed solution

### The rules (all decided, see Open decisions)

1. **Three goals at a time, always.** A player (guest or account) has three slots. A finished goal pays its XP and
   is replaced at once by the next goal from the catalogue, in the same slot.
2. **No expiry, no guilt.** A goal waits for as long as it takes, across days and visits. No countdown, no "ends
   today", no "you missed", no streak. The only time words are the daily cap line (rule 4), which says when goals
   pay again, not what was lost.
3. **Small XP, never on a board.** 5 XP per goal, through progression's `awardBonus` with `unranked: true` and the
   key `goal:<sequence>` (exactly once, like quest XP, ADR-013.5). Goal XP raises the level but is never on a
   leaderboard, so goals cannot be farmed for rank.
4. **A daily cap that is a natural stop.** At most 10 goals are paid per day (50 XP, its own cap beside the 150 XP
   quest cap and the 100 XP computer cap of ADR-025). After the 10th the three current goals stay on screen and stop
   moving until the next day, with one plain line. The day is the daily quests' day (UTC today, the player's local
   day when CC-146 ships), so both reset together.
5. **What counts.** Only games that count for the player under ADR-021 (`creditFor(match, playerId).counted`), the
   player's own forfeit never. Games against computer characters count, within ADR-025's caps.
6. **No goal needs another person online** (ADR-029): every goal can be finished against a computer character. A
   game against a person counts for every goal that does not name a character.
7. **Alongside the daily quests, not inside them.** Quests stay a day snapshot with a sweep (ADR-013, CC-089), and
   their 150 XP cap is unchanged. A goal is never the same as one of today's quests on screen: the picker skips a
   catalogue entry with the same `counts` and `gameId` as a quest of the day.
8. **The same for everyone, never personalised.** The next goal is the next entry of a fixed, shuffled order per
   player (`fnv1a32` and `mulberry32Step` from `questsFor.js`, seeded by player id and the slot's sequence number),
   never chosen from how the player plays. So minors and unknown ages (ADR-028) get exactly the same goals with no
   profiling (ICO children's code standard 12), and the cap gives them the same stopping point.
9. **Swap goal.** Each goal has a Swap goal button that replaces it with the next one, at most 3 swaps a day, no XP
   either way. It keeps a goal for a game the player never plays from sitting there for ever.

### The catalogue

`packages/shared/src/goals.js`, ids stable and stored, never reused. Three pools, one per slot:

| Slot | id                 | Words (English)                     | Counts             | Game / character      | Target | XP  |
| ---- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- | ------ | --- |
| play | `play-rps-2`       | Play 2 games of Rock Paper Scissors | `play`             | rps                   | 2      | 5   |

"Win a round against Rocky" from the brief became "Win a game against Rocky": in our Rock Paper Scissors a game is
best of three rounds, and a "round" goal would read as one throw. Round goals count rounds won inside games
(`rounds-won`, worked out by replaying the stored log with `replayGame`), so "round" means what it says on the
board. "Make 10 chess moves" counts the player's own moves in counted games (the log's move entries by
`playerId`). Any level of a character counts.

### Where it shows, without noise

- **Home and profile: one card, not two.** Behind the flag, the existing quest card (`DailyQuests`, compact on home
  and full on the profile) becomes "Your goals", with "Right now" (the three goals) above "Today's quests" (the daily
  quests as today). One heading, one card, the same place as now. Each goal row: the words, "1 of 2", a progress bar,
  "+5 XP", a Play link to the right page (the character's or the game's lobby) and Swap goal. Visitors without a
  guest see nothing, as today.
- **After a game: one toast at most, silent.** When a finished game completes goals, one reward-category toast
  says it, merged for two or three ("2 goals done. +10 XP"), with the new goal's words. No sound (the quest or
  level chime already plays), no confetti, no effect. It waits with the other reward toasts for the result moment
  (CC-163) and, once they land, goes through the CC-315 notice centre into CC-099's quest beat (beat 6) as one more
  line. This ticket does not edit CC-099's or CC-315's files; it sends `goals:updated` with the `matchId` (ADR-050
  point 1) so they can.
- **Nothing during play.** No goal line on the board or in the match bar. CC-315's quiet period holds the toast.

### Server

- `domain/goals/goalRules.js` (pure): `nextGoal`, `applyMatchToGoals(goals, { match, playerId, day })`, `swap`,
  the cap. No I/O, no clock.
- `domain/goals/goalComponent.js`: `applyMatch({ match, now })`, `getGoals({ playerId, now })`,
  `swapGoal({ playerId, slot, now })`, `transferPlayer`, `exportPlayer`, `erasePlayer`. Version compare-and-set like
  quest days (ADR-013.6), exactly once per match by a 200-key window.
- `data/goalAccessor.js`, `persistence/goalDao.js`, a contract test and an in-memory DAO. Collection
  `player_goals`: `{ playerId, slots: [{ slot, goalId, sequence, progress, startedAt }], day, paidToday,
swapsToday, recentKeys, mergedFrom, version, updatedAt, expiresAt? }`, unique `playerId`. A guest's document gets
  `expiresAt` 30 days after its last change (as ADR-060.5), so CC-098 finds nothing left behind.
- Pipeline: its own background task after gamification (`connectMatchPipeline`), logged as "apply goals" on failure,
  so a goal failure never touches XP or quests.
- Progression: `awardBonus` takes an optional `source` (default `quest`), and goals pass `goal`
  (`XP_SOURCES.GOAL` in `packages/shared/src/progression.js`), so the quest toast does not fire for goal XP.
- Routes: `GET /api/goals` (`private, no-store`) and `POST /api/goals/swap` `{ slot }` (CSRF as every POST, the
  app's rate limit, 400 on an unknown slot). Socket push `goals:updated` `{ goals, matchId? }` to the player's own
  `player:<id>` room.
- ADR-014 participant `goals` after gamification in `server.js` and `testing/testApp.js`.

## Non-goals

- Changing daily quests, their sweep or their 150 XP cap.
- Goals that need another person, a friend, a rating or a leaderboard.
- Reminders or notifications about goals (ADR-031): none, for anyone.
- A goal line during play, a new card on the result screen, or sounds.
- Building CC-099's queue or CC-315's centre. This ticket only sends what they need.

## Rabbit holes

- **Counting rounds and moves.** Read them from the stored match log in one pure function with fixtures for each
  game; do not add fields to matches.
- **Concurrency with two tabs or two games finishing at once.** Reuse the quest day's compare-and-set and the 200-key
  window; never a second write path.
- **Mixing with quest XP keys.** Goal keys are `goal:<sequence>` and never collide with `quest:` keys or match ids
  in progression's window.
- **Days.** Use the gamification calendar (`utcDay`) and nothing new; CC-146 moves both together.

## Acceptance criteria

Each runs in `flagPaths("rolling_goals", …)` where it touches the flag.

- **AC-1** Given a new guest, when they first load `GET /api/goals`, then they get three goals, one from each pool,
  none equal (same `counts` and `gameId`) to a daily quest of the day. Test: `goalRules.test.js` › "starts with
  three goals, one per pool, none the same as a quest of the day"
- **AC-2** Given a goal one game from done, when a counted game finishes it, then 5 XP is awarded once with key
  `goal:<sequence>` and `unranked: true`, the slot holds the next goal, and `goals:updated` carries the `matchId`.
  Applying the match again changes nothing. Test: `goalComponent.test.js` › "a finished goal pays 5 XP once and
  refills its slot"
- **AC-3** Given a game that does not count (ADR-021) or the player's own forfeit, when it is recorded, then no goal
  moves. Test: `goalComponent.test.js` › "games that do not count move no goal"
- **AC-4** Given 10 goals paid today, when another game would finish a goal, then nothing is paid, the goals do not
  move, and the view says `paused: true` until the next day. Test: `goalRules.test.js` › "stops at 10 paid goals
  a day and starts again the next day"
- **AC-5** Given a goal made 9 days ago, when the player comes back, then the same goal and its progress are there.
  Test: `goalRules.test.js` › "a goal never expires"
- **AC-6** Given goal XP, when the boards are read, then it is on no leaderboard, and the level includes it. Test:
  `goals.integration.test.js` › "goal XP raises the level and never reaches a board"
- **AC-7** Given a guest with goals who signs up, when the transfer runs (twice), then the account keeps its own
  goals if it had any, else gets the guest's, today's paid counts add up to at most 10, and the guest document is
  gone. Test: `goalComponent.test.js` › "transfer moves goals once and is idempotent"
- **AC-8** Given an account, when it exports or deletes its data (CC-035), then goals are in the export and erased.
  Test: `accountData.integration.test.js` › "goals are exported and erased"
- **AC-9** Given two players of different ages with the same id sequence, when the next goal is picked, then the
  picker reads only the player id and the sequence. Test: `goalRules.test.js` › "the next goal reads nothing about
  how the player plays"
- **AC-10** Given Swap goal pressed 3 times today, when it is pressed again, then the server answers 409 and the
  button shows the reason. Test: `goalRoutes.test.js` › "a fourth swap in a day is refused" and `Goals.test.jsx` ›
  "the swap button says why it is off"
- **AC-11** Given the flag on and a signed-in or guest player on home, when the page loads, then one card "Your
  goals" shows "Right now" with three rows and "Today's quests" below, and no other new heading. With the flag off
  the card is as today. Test: `Goals.test.jsx` › "one card on home, two sections"
- **AC-12** Given a game that finishes two goals, when the result shows, then exactly one toast, with no sound and
  no effect, says "2 goals done. +10 XP. New ones are on your home page.", and no quest toast fires for goal XP.
  Test: `GamificationToasts.test.jsx` › "one silent toast for goals, none from the quest toast"
- **AC-13** Every English line has a Hinglish twin with "tum" or "tumhara" where it speaks to the player, and none
  says "aap", "today only", "hurry", "miss" or "streak". Test: `goalCopy.test.js` › "every line has a tum twin and
  no pressure words"
- **AC-14** Given the browser on a phone (390 × 844), when a guest wins against Rocky with "Win a game against
  Rocky" on the card, then on home the goal is replaced and the card still fits with no new heading. Test:
  `e2e/goals.spec.js` › "a goal refills after a win against Rocky"

## Instrumentation

Anonymous counts only (ADR-034). The flag's exposure and success counts (ADR-073.6): success is "a goal was paid",
sent from the server with `flags.recordSuccess("rolling_goals", player)`. No new telemetry event, so the
EVENTS.md catalogue does not change; which goals are done and swapped most is a follow-up only if the review asks
for it. CC-313's surface registry gains `toast.goal` (class optional) in the same PR once CC-313 is merged; until
then the toast is counted by CC-174's toast counts.

## Threat model

We add a per-player goals document, a read route, a swap route and a socket push. What can go wrong: farming XP by
self-play (bounded by ADR-021's counted-game rules, the 50 XP cap, and unranked XP); a swap flood (3 a day, app rate
limit, CSRF); reading another player's goals (identity from the session only, no id in the route); a goal push to the
wrong player (the gateway's own `player:<id>` room, as quests). We test each. Good enough: the most one person gains
is 50 XP a day of level, never rank.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** which goal is in each slot, progress, how many were paid and swapped today. About the player only.
- **Retention:** accounts, until the account is deleted (erased with it, CC-035); guests, 30 days after the last
  change (TTL), or moved into the account on sign-in and then deleted.
- **Who sees it:** the player. The console sees only the flag's anonymous exposure and success counts (ADR-073.6,
  ADR-036.2: under 5 is `"<5"`).
- **Minors (ADR-028):** no change, by design: the goals are not personalised, there are no reminders, and the cap is
  a stop for everyone (ICO children's code standards 5 and 12).
- **Defaults:** the flag is off. On, goals are on for everyone in the audience, no setting needed.

## Alternatives considered

- **A fourth quest slot that refills (extend quests).** Rejected: quests are a day snapshot with a sweep bonus and a
  rebuild path (ADR-013.5); a refilling slot breaks both and makes the 150 XP cap harder to reason about.
- **Goals picked from how the player plays** (favourite game). Rejected: profiling, not allowed for minors (ICO
  standard 12), and unknown ages are minors (ADR-028.1).
- **Goals that expire at midnight.** Rejected: that is the guilt the owner ruled out.
- **Bigger XP.** Rejected: goals are for short visits; 5 XP keeps quests the bigger reward.

## Pre-mortem

| Risk                                                        | Mitigation                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Goals felt like chores and the toast was dismissed unread   | One silent toast at most per game, measured by its dismissed share; cut to the card only if over 70 % |
| Goals pulled players away from daily quests                 | Never the same as a quest of the day; quest completions are a guardrail                               |
| A goal for a game the player never plays sat there for ever | Swap goal, 3 a day                                                                                    |
| Players hit the cap at once by playing Rocky quickly        | The cap is a stop with a kind line; at-cap share is a guardrail and tuned                             |
| A goal counted twice when two games ended together          | Compare-and-set and the 200-key window, tested with two games at once                                 |

## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

- Daily quests (CC-089, ADR-013): `packages/shared/src/quests.js` has 12 quests in three slots; `questsFor.js`
  picks one per slot per UTC day from the player id with FNV-1a and mulberry32; `questProgress.js` applies a match
  (counts `play`, `win`, `distinct-games`, `wins-in-a-row`); `gamificationComponent.js` stores a day snapshot
  (`player_quests`), awards each bonus once through `progressionComponent.awardBonus` with `quest:<day>:<id>`, plus
  a 30 XP sweep. At most 150 XP a day. A day's quests never refill: once done, nothing is left until midnight, and
  the smallest quest still takes 1 to 5 games.
- Goals can reuse: the pipeline step after progression, ADR-021's verdict (`creditFor`), the computer rules
  (`hasComputerPlayer`, ADR-025), `awardBonus` with `unranked`, the ADR-014 participant shape, and the match log
  (moves and rounds by `playerId`) for counting.
- Web: `DailyQuests.jsx` is the compact card on home (only when a session is ready) and the full list on the profile.
  `GamificationToasts.jsx` toasts "Quest complete! +N XP" on every `progress:updated` with `source: "quest"`, which
  is the only source `awardBonus` emits today, so goal XP needs its own source or it would fire the quest toast.
- Gap: nothing small that refills, and nothing that suggests what to play next.

### 2. How other products do it (read 2026-10-22)

- **Jetpack Joyride (Halfbrick, 2011): three missions that refill.** "At any time in the game, the player is provided
  with three missions"; finishing one gives stars toward a level and "a fresh mission appears"; missions can be
  skipped with coins. The pattern we take: always three, replaced at once, a skip. What we leave: the paid skip and
  the stars economy. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jetpack_Joyride (Gameplay).
- **Duolingo daily quests (and our own CC-089 research):** three quests a day with a small reward and a visible
  reset. Good for daily habit, and what our daily quests already copy; their reset time is the pressure the owner
  ruled out for goals. Sources: our CC-089 research (Brawl Stars and Duolingo); Duolingo's streak research,
  https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/, on why daily resets drive return and also stress.
- **ICO Age appropriate design code, standard 5 (Detrimental use of data):** names "reward loops, continuous
  scrolling, notifications and auto-play features which encourage users to continue playing" as features to avoid
  using personal data for, and asks for ways to stop "at any time" and to "present options to continue playing …
  neutrally". Source:
  https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/5-detrimental-use-of-data/
  and standard 12 (profiling), already cited in CC-152.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

Three refilling goals fit our short visits and our solo-first reality (ADR-029: every goal works against a character).
Keeping them beside quests keeps ADR-013's rebuild and cap story intact. Tiny unranked XP and a daily stop make it a
pull, not a treadmill: the goal is "one more small thing", not "keep going". Not personalising keeps minors safe
without a second code path, and one card keeps home as calm as it is now.
