Shaped with [CC-354](CC-354.md) and [CC-355](CC-355.md) after the owner lifted the feature freeze for these three
on 2026-10-22 (ADR-082). We take the general pattern (a live sign that others are here), never another game's
theme, art or words.

## Problem

Meera opens the home page from a friend's WhatsApp link at 9 pm. Twenty people are in games at that moment, but the
page looks the same as at 4 am: nothing says anyone else is here, so "play a person" feels like a gamble and she
picks a computer character or leaves. ADR-029 planned "N playing now" behind a gate (a 7 day median of 50 people
online at that hour), which would keep it hidden at friends launch even on a busy evening.

## Job to be done

When I land on ClassiCade or a game's page, I want to see, honestly, that other people are playing right now, so I
trust the site is alive and try a game.

## Appetite

S (up to 2 agent-hours) for the server count and route, plus the two lines on the web: S overall, at most M. **Cut
line**, dropped first: the per-game count on the lobbies (home keeps the total); the 60 s refresh in an open tab
(the count loads once per page). Never cut: the 10 floor, rounding, no ids, the server cache, the flag.

## Proposed solution

### What is counted

**People in a game now:** distinct people (never computer characters, never spectators) seated in a room whose game
is on (`status: playing`), in total and per game. A person in two games counts once in the total and once in each
game. This is "playing", not "online": a tab left on the home page is not counted (Steam keeps the same line
between "online" and "in game"). It is read from the `rooms` collection, so a second instance (CC-061) shows the same
number without shared memory.

### How it is refreshed

- **Server:** `liveCountComponent` keeps the last answer in memory for 30 s. The first request after it is stale
  runs one aggregate on `rooms` (match playing, unwind seats, drop computer ids, group by game, distinct player);
  requests during that read wait for the same promise (single flight). No timer runs when nobody asks. At most one
  read per 30 s whatever the traffic; at the ADR-035 cap of 2000 open rooms the read is small.
- **Browser:** the line loads once after the page's first paint (never in the first route's critical path), and
  again every 60 s only while the tab is visible. A failed load shows nothing (no error text: the line is
  decoration, and C4's rule is for things the player asked for).

### What leaves the server

`GET /api/live` answers `{ total?: number, games?: { [gameId]: number } }` with `Cache-Control: private, max-age=30`.
A number is present only when it is **10 or more**, and it is **rounded down to a multiple of 5** (17 becomes 15).
Below 10 the key is absent, so an exact small number never leaves the server (ADR-034, ADR-036.2). No id, name,
room code or seat of any kind. With the flag off for the asker, the answer is `{}`.

### Where it shows, and the exact copy (CONTRACT.md §7, Hinglish twin with "tum")

| Where                                               | English (shown)              | Screen reader text                        | Hinglish twin                     |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Home, right end of the "Games" heading row          | 15+ people playing now       | 15 or more people are playing a game now. | Abhi 15+ log khel rahe hain       |
| A game's lobby, in the banner under the game's name | 10+ people playing Chess now | 10 or more people are playing Chess now.  | Abhi 10+ log Chess khel rahe hain |
| Under 10, loading, an error, or the flag off        | nothing (no line, no space)  | nothing                                   | nothing                           |

The "+" makes the rounded number true. No "only", no "join them!", no "hurry", no animation, no count that ticks up.
The line is muted body text with no icon (C6), is not a button, and is `aria-live="off"`, so a refresh is never
announced. Both places sit in a row whose height does not depend on the line (the heading row, the banner with its
art), so it causes no layout shift. The page shows English, as home and lobbies do today; the Hinglish twin is in
`liveCountCopy.js` for the language switch and the C9 check ("log" and "khel rahe hain" speak about others, so no
"tum" is needed in these lines).

### Server pieces

- `domain/presence/liveCount.js` (pure): `visibleCounts({ total, games }, { floor: 10, step: 5 })`.
- `domain/presence/liveCountComponent.js`: the 30 s cache and single flight, injected `now`.
- `data/roomAccessor.js`: `countPeopleInGames()`, and `persistence/roomDao.js` its aggregate, in the room DAO
  contract (in memory and MongoDB). Computer seats are recognised by the bot id shape (`models/playerIdShape.js`).
- `service/http/liveRoutes.js`: `GET /api/live`, reads the flag for the asker, no session required (a visitor gets
  it only when the flag is On).

### ADR-029

ADR-082 point 6 amends ADR-029's "N playing now" row (recorded under ADR-029 in this shaping PR): the 7 day median
of 50 is replaced by the live floor of 10, rounded to 5, with "Nothing" before it. The nightly gate (CC-228) is not
needed for this row.

## Non-goals

- Who is playing, avatars, recent wins, or any list (recent wins keep their own ADR-029 gate).
- "Online now", friends' presence (CC-052 owns presence for friends), or counts in the room or during play.
- A push of the count over the socket.
- Changing ADR-029's other rows.

## Rabbit holes

- **Counting by sockets.** Tempting (the gateway already has `counts()`), but it counts open tabs on any page,
  differs per instance, and would say "playing" about people reading the rules. Rooms are the truth.
- **Flicker around 10.** Rounding to 5 and the 30 s cache make it change at most every 30 s; no hysteresis needed in
  the first build.
- **Bundle.** The hook and line are a few hundred bytes; they must not pull a library into the first route.

## Instrumentation

Anonymous counts only (ADR-034). Flag exposure and success counts (ADR-073.6): exposure when the line is shown,
success "a game started from the page where the line was shown", sent with `api.sendFlagSuccess`. No new event. The
server logs one line per read failure ("reading the live count failed"), with no ids.

## Threat model

A new public, unauthenticated read. What can go wrong: a flood that hammers MongoDB (one read per 30 s whatever the
traffic, the app-wide rate limit, ADR-035); leaking who plays (no ids, names, codes or seats in the answer; numbers
under 10 never leave the server; rounding to 5); inferring one small game from the total minus the others
(rounding blurs each by up to 4; accepted, as ADR-036.3, since it says how many people, never who); a fake number
(computed only on the server from rooms). We test each. Good enough: the endpoint can only ever say "at least N
people are in games", with N of 10 or more.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** a count of people in games, in total and per game. About nobody in particular.
- **Retention:** the last answer, in memory, for 30 s. Nothing stored.
- **Who sees it:** anyone who opens home or a lobby while the flag is on for them, only at 10 or more.
- **Minors (ADR-028):** unchanged: minors are counted like everyone, and nothing about any player is shown.
- **Defaults:** the flag is off; under 10 nothing shows.

## Alternatives considered

- **Connected sockets** (the gateway's `counts()`): cheaper still, but counts tabs, not people in games, and is per
  instance. Rejected for honesty and CC-061.
- **ADR-029's 7 day median of 50.** Rejected for this row: it hides a true "20 playing" on a good evening at friends
  launch, and the live floor already prevents an empty-site signal.
- **Exact numbers.** Rejected: an exact 11 next to a game total lets anyone watch small changes; rounding costs
  nothing.
- **A socket push.** Rejected: a count does not need to be live to the second, and home has no socket on its first
  route (CC-333).

## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

- No player-facing count exists. The server logs a `room gauge` every minute (`server.js`: rooms per status from
  `roomComponent.countRooms`, and `gateway.counts()`, connections and connected players from `socketAdmission.js`,
  in memory, per instance). The console has room counts (ADR-036, small groups hidden).
- Rooms are in MongoDB (`rooms`, at most 2000 open, ADR-035) with seats by player id; computer players have their
  own id kind (`bot_…`, ADR-025).
- ADR-029 gated "N playing now" behind a nightly 7 day median of 50 online at that hour (CC-228, not built), with
  "Nothing" before it. PRODUCT_VISION §6.0 and CC-259 forbid fake activity and "0 playing now".
- Since CC-333 home has no socket on its first route, so a count by HTTP fits it best. The first route is about
  135 kB of gzip against the 150 kB budget (CC-322, CC-333).

### 2. How other products do it (read 2026-10-22)

- **Lichess** shows "%s players" and "%s games in play" on its home page (lila translation keys `nbPlayers`,
  `nbGamesInPlay`, and "%s playing" for a pool). Source:
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lichess-org/lila/master/translation/source/site.xml and
  https://github.com/lichess-org/lila. We take: two plain numbers, players and games, no faces.
- **Steam Charts** separates "Players online" from "In game" per title. Source: https://store.steampowered.com/charts/.
  We take: "playing" means in a game, not on the site.
- **Chess.com's** logged-out home page shows a member total ("Join 250+ million players"), not a live count. Source:
  https://www.chess.com/ (read 2026-10-22). We take: a small site should show a live number only when it is
  meaningful; a lifetime total would be misleading for us.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

At friends-launch scale, a live "15+ people playing now" on a busy evening is the honest social proof we have, and
nothing at quiet hours is better than a small number. Counting people in games from rooms is true, cheap with a 30 s
cache, and works with more than one instance. A floor of 10 and rounding to 5 keep it anonymous by construction, and
placing it in rows that already exist keeps home calm and the first route inside its budget.
