## Research (written before any code)

### 1. Did CC-275 already fix it? Reproduced at 412 px (Pixel 7) after a win

CC-275 (#96, merged 2026-10-18) made the room's seat row wrap and thought CC-270 was "most likely the same row".
Checked on 2026-10-19 with a throwaway Playwright probe on the `mobile` project (Pixel 7, 412×915), local e2e server
on `E2E_PORT=4982`. The probe signs up, opens the lobby, creates a best-of-1 Rock Paper Scissors room, a guest joins,
the player wins, and a second win gives the level-up. A `requestAnimationFrame` loop in the page records the largest
`document.documentElement.scrollWidth` seen on every frame, so a burst that lasts 300 ms is not missed.

| Build                                    | Name                     | Room waiting | Room playing | Result after the win (every frame) | Level-up (every frame) |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------ | ------------ | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| `768ce9c` (main just before #96, CC-275) | `riya_` + 10 digits (15) | 452 px       | 435 px       | 452 px                             | not reached            |
| `b173cd4` (main today, after #96)        | `riya_` + 10 digits (15) | 412 px       | 412 px       | 412 px                             | 412 px                 |
| `b173cd4` (main today, after #96)        | `W`×10 + 10 digits (20)  | 412 px       | 412 px       | 412 px                             | 412 px                 |

On the old build the wide elements were the room's seat row (`li._seat_…`, 420 px inside a 412 px screen) and
everything that grows with it (the room's header, `main`, the site header with the Menu button pushed to 436 px).
No effect element was ever past the edge. So CC-270's "451 px after a win" was the seat row of the result screen,
the same cause as CC-275, and **CC-275 fixed it**. The ticket guessed confetti or the level-up burst. The
measurements say otherwise:

1. `art/effects/effects.module.css` `.overlay` is `position: fixed; inset: 0; overflow: hidden`. Every confetti
   piece and the level-up rays are inside it and clipped to the screen.
2. Fixed boxes do not add to the page's scroll width. The proof is in the same run at 360 px: the toasts slide in
   from 1.5rem to the right (`toast-in`) and reached `right = 368` on a 360 px screen, while `scrollWidth` stayed 360.
3. The result banner's stamp (`scale(1.6)` at the start) and the XP pop stay inside the result card at 360, 390 and
   412 px.

What the probe did find at 360 px, with a 20 character name, belongs to CC-256 (the same branch): home is 376 px wide
and the profile 612 px wide. That is the long name, not an effect.

### 2. How others keep effects and mobile layouts inside the screen

Sources read on 2026-10-19.

1. **canvas-confetti** (github.com/catdad/canvas-confetti, `src/confetti.js`): the library draws on one canvas with
   `position: fixed` and `pointer-events: none` over the window. The same idea as our `.overlay`: an effect lives in
   a fixed layer the size of the viewport, never in the page flow, so it cannot change the page's width.
2. **MDN, `contain`** (last modified 2026-09-07): with `contain: paint`, "if a descendant overflows the containing
   element's bounds, then that descendant will be clipped". It is a way to keep an animated part inside its box. Our
   overlay already clips with `overflow: hidden`, which does the same for a fixed layer.
3. **MDN, `overflow-x`** (last modified 2026-04-20): `clip` clips like `hidden` but "forbids all scrolling, including
   programmatic scrolling". `overflow-x: clip` on `body` is a common way to hide sideways scrolling. It hides the
   symptom and keeps the cause (content cut off on the right), so we do not use it.
4. **MDN, viewport meta** (last modified 2026-09-23): without `initial-scale` "the browser may zoom out when content
   overflows the viewport". We have `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0`, yet Chrome on Android still widens the
   layout viewport to the content, which is the 451 px the ticket saw.
5. **MDN, `clamp()`** (last modified 2026-08-12): fluid sizes between a minimum and a maximum. Our banner already
   steps down below 30rem, so no change is needed for width.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. Close CC-270 as fixed by CC-275, with the measurements above, and no change to the effects.
2. Add the guard it asked for: a browser check at 360×800, 390×844 and 412×915 that the page is never wider than the
   screen on each main screen, including the result after a win with a level-up, sampled on every frame while the
   effects play. It is shared with CC-256 and uses a 20 character name.
3. Never hide overflow with `overflow-x: hidden` or `clip` on the page: the check must see a real overflow.

## Root cause

The seat row in the room, a flex row that could not wrap, with a long name, its tags and "connected" (see CC-275).
On the result screen it is still there, so a win was where it showed. Fixed by CC-275 in #96. Not an effect.

## Fix

Nothing more in the app. The guard test is `e2e/layout-width.spec.js` (with CC-256).

## Verification

1. Before CC-275 (`768ce9c`), 412×915, 15 character name: room waiting 452 px, room playing 435 px, result 452 px.
   On main (`b173cd4`): 412 px on every screen and every frame, with a 15 and a 20 character name, through the
   win's confetti and the level-up burst.
2. The guard, `e2e/layout-width.spec.js`, records the page width on every frame. To prove it sees an effect that
   lasts under a second, the level-up overlay was made `position: absolute` and its rays moved 40vw right for one
   local run: the check failed at "result after a win with a level-up: widest 596 px", with the rays and the toast
   region named. Reverted.
3. Results of the browser runs are in CC-256's Verification.
