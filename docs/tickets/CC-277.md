## Research (written before any code)

Sources were read on 2026-10-19 unless another date is given. "General knowledge" carries a confidence level.

### 1. How our code does it today

1. The profile's match history (`apps/web/src/pages/ProfilePage/ProfilePage.jsx`, `GET /api/matches`) lists each
   match with the opponents' display names, a computer labelled as one (ADR-025). There is no button on it.
2. A room is the only way to play a person again. Rematch works for 30 minutes after a game (`rematchWindowMs` in
   `roomLifetimes.js`), then the room closes and the two players have no way to find each other unless one of them
   still has a chat with the other.
3. Persistent challenges (CC-139) are not built, so there is nothing to send to a past opponent.

### 2. How others do it

1. **Steam** ("Recently played with" in the friends list) and **Xbox** ("Recent players") list people from your
   recent games, so a player can add or invite them without searching by name (general knowledge, high confidence).
2. **Chess.com** offers recent opponents when you start a challenge (general knowledge, medium confidence).
3. **Roblox** "Trusted Connections" (17 July 2025, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roblox) draw the line for a 13 to
   17 year old at people they can prove they know in real life (a QR code scanned in person, or phone contacts).
4. **Lichess** lets each player choose who may challenge them ("Never", "Only friends", "If registered" by default,
   "Always", `modules/pref/src/main/Pref.scala` in https://github.com/lichess-org/lila).

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

1. A "Play again with" list on the profile (and later home): the last 20 different people from the player's own
   match history in the last 30 days, newest first. It reads `matches` only, so nothing new is stored.
2. Each row has "Challenge again", which sends a CC-139 challenge. The other player must accept. It is an
   asked-for message under ADR-031 (at most 1 per opponent every 15 minutes).
3. Never listed: computers, the player themself, anyone blocked either way (CC-044), deleted players
   ("Deleted player" stand-ins, ADR-048.3).
4. The client names a match id, never a player id (OWASP IDOR, as in CC-044).
5. **Minors (owner to confirm, ADR-051 point 9):** the list shows for everyone, but a minor, or a player whose
   opponent is a minor, can challenge again only someone they met in a room joined by a link, never a quick match
   stranger. Sharing a link by hand is our "know them in real life".
6. A player can switch off being challenged by past opponents in CC-278.

## Why

People you know are the retention loop (PRODUCT_VISION.md §4.6), and today a good opponent is lost 30 minutes after
the game.

## Scope

- In: the list, Challenge again through CC-139, the block and minor rules, the CC-278 switch.
- Out: friends (CC-052), presence, search by name.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The list shows up to 20 people from the last 30 days, never computers, blocked players or the player themself.
- [ ] Challenge again sends a CC-139 challenge, refused for a blocked pair and for the minor rule, with a neutral
      message.
- [ ] Requests name a match id only.
- [ ] Tests cover the change. `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm test` pass.
- [ ] Docs updated: BACKLOG, PROGRESS.
