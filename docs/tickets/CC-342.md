## Problem

`chessEngine.test.js` › "ends random games exactly when and how a full chess.js replay would" failed main's CI on
4d867e9 with "Test timed out in 30000ms". Root cause: it plays ten seeded games, 1,886 half moves in all, through the
engine and a chess.js replay. Under coverage instrumentation that takes about 26 s on an M4 laptop (2.2 s without
coverage), and the whole file took 30.6 s on CI's green run before, so a busy runner pushed it past the limit. Nothing
in the engine got slower, so the test had no headroom.

## Fix

Play three seeds instead of ten: seed 10 (the only one that ends by the fifty-move rule, 428 half moves) and seeds 2
and 12 (repetition after 107 and 67). The test still proves both kinds of ending and the same position checks.
Research: per-seed game lengths measured with chess.js alone; the time limit is unchanged and nothing is retried.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given the coverage run, when the test runs, then it takes well under its 30 s limit (16 s locally under
  coverage, down from 26 s) and still sees both "fifty-move-rule" and "threefold-repetition".

## Test

The test itself, timed under coverage before (26 s) and after (16 s).
