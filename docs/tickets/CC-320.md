## Problem

The owner wants a premium, consistent arcade site and has handed the design decisions over. Before any redesign
there was no evidence-based picture of the screens as a whole: CC-155's baselines cover 16 screens, CC-183's axe tour
covers the screens the browser tests reach, and nothing measured responsiveness at tablet width, the bundle against
its budget or the consistency of components across pages.

## Fix

A documented audit, no app code: [`docs/design/AUDIT-2026-10.md`](../design/AUDIT-2026-10.md).

**Research note.**

- **How our code does it today.** Look and feel rules live in ADR-012, ADR-016, ADR-050, ADR-052 and
  [LOOK_AND_FEEL.md](../domains/LOOK_AND_FEEL.md). The design contract (CC-314) is being built on its branch. Reviews
  every two weeks (CC-316) are at spec review and will write to `docs/ux/reviews/`. This audit is their first
  baseline.
- **Sources checked.**
  - Nielsen's severity ratings for usability problems (NN/g, 1994), for the 1 to 4 scale.
  - WCAG 2.2 AA, with 2.5.8 for target size, through axe-core and the CC-183 rule set.
  - Lighthouse 13.5 in mobile mode.
  - The 150 kB first-route budget from vision §6.11.

**Method.**

- 77 states captured with scripted Playwright against the local e2e server, at 390×844, 768×1024 and 1280×800, in
  light and dark: 451 screenshots on `pr-evidence/audit-2026-10/`.
- On every state:
  - axe;
  - overflow;
  - tap-target size;
  - reduced motion;
  - type sizes.
- On the key states: keyboard walks and hover checks.
- `npm run build:web`, with the main chunk split by its source map.
- Lighthouse in mobile mode on home, the chess lobby and the daily puzzle.
- A static read of the CSS modules and components.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given the audit file, when a reader opens it, then it has a per-page table of findings, each with a
  Nielsen severity, an impact score and evidence.
- **AC-2** Given the audit file, then it names the cross-cutting root causes and the top 15 improvements. Each says
  what is already planned in CC-314, CC-315, CC-099, CC-154 or CC-28x.
- **AC-3** Given the audit file, then it records the gzip size of every chunk and the main chunk against the 150 kB
  budget, plus the Lighthouse mobile numbers.
- **AC-4** Given the audit file, then every screenshot it cites links to `pr-evidence/audit-2026-10/` and shows test
  accounts only.
