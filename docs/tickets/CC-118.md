## Why

SEC-17: signing up with an email that already has an account answers "That email can't be used", so anyone can
find out whether an email has a ClassiCade account. Login already answers every failure the same way after the same
work (`verifyAgainstDummy`).

## Current behaviour (before this ticket)

1. Taken email in `accounts`: 409, `CONFLICT`, "That email can't be used", `details.field: "email"`, after hashing
   the password (the hash runs before the insert that fails).
2. Email of a legacy player (CC-078): the same 409 and message, but answered before hashing, so it was about one
   scrypt hash (tens of milliseconds) faster. The timing told a legacy email apart from a new account's.
3. Taken username: 409 "That username is taken" on `username`. Usernames are public on every room and leaderboard,
   so this reveals nothing new.
4. Sign-up limit: the shared 20 requests per 15 minutes per address for all account routes.

## Changes

1. Timing equalised: the password is hashed first in every case, so a legacy email and an account's email are
   refused after the same work with the same body. A test pins that both refusals hash once and answer the same
   message and field.
2. New sign-up limit per address: 10 sign-ups an hour (`signupPerIp` in `authRoutes.js`), on top of the shared 20
   per 15 minutes. Checking a long list of emails now takes a very long time or very many addresses.
3. Nothing else changes in the answers.

## Known issue note (to copy into KNOWN_ISSUES.md as the SEC-17 note when the board is updated)

> SEC-17 (CC-118, partly fixed): sign-up still answers "That email can't be used" for an email that has an
> account. Until email verification ships (CC-033) this cannot be hidden: without an email to send, the only way to
> tell the person why their sign-up failed is to say so on the page. A uniform answer ("check your email") would
> leave a real new player with no account and no explanation. Mitigations now: the same answer and the same work
> for every kind of taken email (legacy or new), 10 sign-ups an hour per address, and login reveals nothing. When
> CC-033 lands, sign-up answers "check your email" for every valid request, and the email says either "confirm
> your account" or "someone tried to sign up with your email".

## Trade-off

1. Hiding the duplicate case now would need either a fake success (the player thinks they have an account, then
   cannot log in) or a vague failure for everyone. Both hurt every honest player to slow down a check that an
   attacker can already make, slowly, through the reset flow most sites have.
2. An email alone is low value here: the app stores no other personal data on the account and login gives nothing
   away. The real harm is a list of emails to target with password guessing, which CC-117 limits.

## Verification

1. `accountComponent.test.js`: "signup does not tell a legacy email from an account's" (one hash each, same
   message and field).
2. `authRoutes.test.js`: "limits sign-ups per address on their own" (the third sign-up is refused, login from the
   same address still works). The browser test server raises `signupPerIp` because every test player shares one
   address (`e2e/app.mjs`).
3. The existing e2e "a taken email or username is explained on the right field" still passes.
