## Problem

Half our players' phones are in light mode (the OS decides, ADR-052 point 5). In light mode ClassiCade loses itself:
pale lavender on white with almost no depth, a win card in brown and olive with a brown "VICTORY!", a winning line
in brown-gold on lavender, medals whose silver is slate in light and white in dark, and a cream and green chess board
that has nothing of the arcade and is the brightest thing on the page in dark mode (AUDIT-2026-10, RC-7, findings
`result-rps-win 1280-light`, `leaderboard-account 1280-light`, `playing-chess 768-light`). Dark mode looks like the
brand; light looks like a form app.

## Job to be done

When my phone is in light mode, I want the same neon arcade I see in dark mode, bright and deep rather than pale, so
the game feels like ClassiCade whichever way my phone is set.

## Appetite

S (up to 2 agent-hours). Token values and the medal art's tokens only. **Cut line:** the leaderboard medal border
change (it then follows the medal tokens on its next touch). The gold scale, the board tokens, the elevation and
every contrast check are never cut.

## Proposed solution

A values-only change in the token files (CONTRACT.md: "a palette refresh touches `palette.css` only, a deeper glow
`elevation.css` only"), plus new tokens for the gold scale, the medals and the board. Applying the new board, win
and glow tokens inside the boards and the result is CC-327's work, which follows in Wave 2b; values that existing
CSS already reads (the background, the gold, the glows, the board squares) change the screens at once.

### What keeps the arcade feel in light mode

1. The page is a deeper lavender with a visible grid, so white cards float on a lit arcade floor.
2. Every resting card has a 1 px primary-tinted edge (in `--shadow-sm`) and hover and dialogs glow (`--glow-primary`).
3. Wins are gold-bright with ink text, not brown; the winning line and medals use gold-bright with an ink rim.
4. The chess board is the night board, framed in ink with `--glow-board`; Tic-Tac-Toe and RPS boards get
   `--glow-board` when CC-327 frames them.
5. The hero gradient and neon inks are unchanged: the hero already carries the brand in both themes.

## Research (written before any code)

1. **How our code does it today.** `palette.css` and `elevation.css` (CC-314) hold every value; light has
   `#f4f3ff` and white, shadows at 8 to 10 % with no edge, gold `#8a5a00` (brown, picked for 4.5:1), tier colours
   doubling as medal fills, and a fixed cream and green board in both themes.
2. **How others do it.** Material 3's tonal palettes (m3.material.io, general knowledge, high confidence) use a
   tinted background below white surfaces for elevation; Apple's Human Interface Guidelines describe gold and
   other bright hues as fills with dark text, not as text. Chess.com and lichess offer many board themes, including
   purple and blue ones (general knowledge, high confidence); a board is an identity choice. WCAG 1.4.3 and 1.4.11
   set the 4.5:1 and 3:1 limits used.
3. **What is best for ClassiCade.** Our identity is neon on night. In light mode the night lives in the board, the
   hero and the glows, and gold becomes a fill with ink text so it stays bright. Values only, so nothing moves and
   every screen improves at once.
