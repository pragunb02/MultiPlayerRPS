## Problem

A player can have 4 or more challenges open at once, not 3 (`CHALLENGE_LIMITS.openPerSender`), when they make them
in the same millisecond. Found while fixing CC-336.

### Root cause

`challengeComponent.create` stored the new challenge and then read the sender's challenges back. It sorted them
by `createdAt` and then by id, and refused the new one only when it was not among the first 3 open. Two
challenges made in the same millisecond have the same `createdAt`, so only their random ids order them. A
challenge made after three others, one after another or at the same moment, could sort ahead of an older one by
its id, count as within the limit, and stay, while the older one, already let through, stayed too. With 2 open, A
could also read the list before B was stored, and B could then sort ahead of A. On the real MongoDB DAO, the new
test made 4 open one after another in the same millisecond, and 13 at once ended with 4 open.

## Fix

Each open challenge holds one of its sender's 3 places for open challenges, `openSlot` 0 to 2, and a unique
partial index on `{ fromId, openSlot }` (`open_slot`) means only one challenge can hold each place. The limit now
holds in one write, whatever the order of reads and writes.

- `challengeDao.insert` reports which key it hit: `DuplicateKey` with `field` "challengeId" or "openSlot".
  `freeSlots({ fromId, at })` lets go of the places of the sender's challenges that can never be open again:
  expired, declined, withdrawn, or taken with their room made. A hold whose room is not made yet keeps its place,
  since `release` opens it again if the room cannot be made. `movePlayer` moves a guest's places to the account,
  except a place the account holds already; that challenge moves without a place.
- `challengeAccessor.add(challenge, { openSlots })` lets go of the finished places, then tries each place in
  turn. It throws `ConflictError` when every place is held. The component turns that into the usual 409 with
  `reason: "open-challenges"`.
- The component no longer decides the limit by sorting. It still refuses a new challenge when more than 3 are
  open. That happens only to an account that had open challenges when a guest with open ones signed in to it, as
  before.
- The in-memory DAO does the same, under the same contract tests.

**Research.** How our code does it now: every other once-only rule in the challenge DAO is a conditional update
(`claim`, `end`, `setRoom`), so two takers can never both win (ADR-069). The open limit was the only rule decided
by reading and sorting after the write. Options: a per-player counter with a conditional `$inc` would also need a
`$inc` back on every way a challenge stops being open, and expiry happens with no write at all, so the counter
would drift. A unique index on a fixed set of places makes the database refuse a second holder. Expired places are
let go when the next one is made, and that can never be wrong, because an expired challenge never opens again.
This follows MongoDB's guidance for uniqueness under concurrency: a unique index, not a check before or after the
write (https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/core/index-unique/, https://www.mongodb.com/docs/manual/core/index-partial/).

**Left as it is:** the "one challenge to the same person every ten minutes" rule still uses the sort after the
write. It has the same kind of race for two challenges to one person in the same millisecond. Its cost is one
extra challenge to someone already challenged.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given a player with 3 open challenges, when they make a fourth in the same millisecond, then it is
  refused with 409 `open-challenges`, and 3 stay open.
- **AC-2** Given a player with 1 open challenge, when they make 12 at once, then exactly 2 are made and 3 are open.
- **AC-3** Given a player at the limit, when one of their challenges is withdrawn or expires, then they can make
  one more, and never more than 3 are open.

## Test

`challengeLimit.integration.test.js` runs the component on the real MongoDB DAO, with ids that sort each new
challenge ahead of the older ones. Before the fix all three tests failed: the fourth was made, 13 at once gave 4
open, and expiry left 4 open. After the fix all pass. The DAO contract (MongoDB and in-memory) covers the unique
places, `freeSlots` and `movePlayer`. The accessor and component tests cover a full set of places and an account
over the limit after a guest signed in. `challengeDao.js`, `challengeAccessor.js` and `challengeComponent.js`
have every line and function covered.
