## Why

A game whose opponent closed the tab never ended, a player could stall a game for ever by not moving, and a game
nobody started still waited.

## Rules (in `roomLifetimes.js` and the engines, all overridable only by tests)

1. **Reconnect grace, 60 s.** A player who loses their connection in a game still on has a minute to come back. A
   minute covers a phone lock screen, a switch from mobile data to wifi, and a server restart (a deploy takes a few
   seconds, the free plan's cold start is not a concern because nobody is playing then). After it the player still
   there may **claim the win** (`room:claim-win`). The claim is their choice, never automatic, because a friend
   may prefer to wait. The opponent loses by forfeit, which fair play already judges (ADR-021).
2. **Idle limits per game.** Rock Paper Scissors 30 s per choice (choosing a hand takes a moment), Tic Tac Toe 60 s
   per move (nine cells leave little to think about). Chess has no extra limit: its clock decides. The turn counts
   from the last thing that happened in the game. Players due to move who pass the limit lose as `idle`, and if
   every player is due it is a draw. Fair play treats `idle` like a forfeit, so stalling before two moves each
   earns nothing.
3. **First move abort, 60 s.** A player due to move who has not made a first move in this game within a minute
   aborts it: the room shows "Game aborted", nothing is recorded (no match, no XP), the series is unchanged and a
   rematch stays possible. This also covers chess before White's first move, when the clocks do not run yet, and
   Black's first reply.

## Design

- `domain/rooms/roomPresence.js`: `graceUntil`, `turnLimit` and `nextDue`. The room's `dueAt` is the earliest of the
  chess flag, the idle limit, a request expiring and a seat release, so the every second sweep handles them all.
- The engine contract gained the optional `turnLimitMs`. The replay accepts `idle` log entries.
- RoomView: `game.turnEndsAt` drives the "thinking 0:24" chip, and each seat's `graceUntil` the reconnecting
  countdown and the Claim the win card (`components/ClaimWin`).
- Games now carry `number` (from 1), because an aborted game does not add to the series.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Claim after the grace period, refused before it, refused against an away opponent.
- [x] Idle losses, idle draws, first move aborts that record nothing.
- [x] Unit tests (`roomComponent.presence.test.js`, `fairPlay.test.js`), MongoDB integration (abort records no
      match), browser test for the claim (`restart-presence.spec.js`).
