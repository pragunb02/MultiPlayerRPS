## Why

A game whose opponent closed the tab never ended, a player could stall a game for ever by not moving, and a game
nobody started still waited.

## Research

**How our code did it before.** A game whose opponent closed the tab never ended, a player could stall for ever by
not moving, and a game nobody started waited until the room's own lifetime ran out (CC-101).

**How other products do it.**

- Lichess aborts a game in which a player makes no first move in time (nothing is rated), and when an opponent leaves
  a running game it offers "Claim victory", "Call draw" or wait after a short countdown, never ending the game on its
  own ([lichess.org/faq](https://lichess.org/faq), "game aborted" and "claim victory").
- Chess.com aborts a game with no first move and gives a disconnected player a timer before the game is lost by
  abandonment ([support.chess.com](https://support.chess.com/), "What happens if I disconnect").
- Turn based sites without clocks (Board Game Arena) give each move a time limit and let the waiting player end a
  stalled game once it passes.

**What is best for ClassiCade.** A 60 s reconnect grace (covers a phone lock screen, a network switch and a deploy
restart), after which the player still there may claim the win, never automatic because friends may wait. Idle limits
per game where there is no clock (30 s for an RPS choice, 60 s for a TTT move, chess keeps its clock), and a first
move abort after 60 s that records nothing, as Lichess and Chess.com do. Fair play (ADR-021) treats idle like a
forfeit, so stalling earns nothing.

## Rules (in `roomLifetimes.js` and the engines, all overridable only by tests)

1. **Reconnect grace, 60 s.** A player who loses their connection in a game still on has a minute to come back. A
   minute covers a phone lock screen, a switch from mobile data to wifi, and a server restart (a deploy takes a few
   seconds, the free plan's cold start is not a concern because nobody is playing then). After it the player still
   there may **claim the win** (`room:claim-win`). The claim is their choice, never automatic, because a friend
   may prefer to wait. The opponent loses by forfeit, which fair play already judges (ADR-021).
2. **Idle limits per game.** Rock Paper Scissors 30 s per choice (choosing a hand takes a moment), Tic Tac Toe 60 s
   per move (nine cells leave little to think about). Chess has no extra limit: its clock decides. The turn counts
   from the last thing that happened in the game. Players due to move who pass the limit lose as `idle`, and if
   every player is due it is a draw. Fair play treats `idle` like a forfeit, so stalling before two moves each
   earns nothing.
3. **First move abort, 60 s.** A player due to move who has not made a first move in this game within a minute
   aborts it: the room shows "Game aborted", nothing is recorded (no match, no XP), the series is unchanged and a
   rematch stays possible. This also covers chess before White's first move, when the clocks do not run yet, and
   Black's first reply.

## Design

- `domain/rooms/roomPresence.js`: `graceUntil`, `turnLimit` and `nextDue`. The room's `dueAt` is the earliest of the
  chess flag, the idle limit, a request expiring and a seat release, so the every second sweep handles them all.
- The engine contract gained the optional `turnLimitMs`. The replay accepts `idle` log entries.
- RoomView: `game.turnEndsAt` drives the "thinking 0:24" chip, and each seat's `graceUntil` the reconnecting
  countdown and the Claim the win card (`components/ClaimWin`).
- Games now carry `number` (from 1), because an aborted game does not add to the series.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Claim after the grace period, refused before it, refused against an away opponent.
- [x] Idle losses, idle draws, first move aborts that record nothing.
- [x] Unit tests (`roomComponent.presence.test.js`, `fairPlay.test.js`), MongoDB integration (abort records no
      match), browser test for the claim (`restart-presence.spec.js`).

## PR #67 review fixes

- **Idle limits ran on through a restart and reconnects.** `turnLimit` counted from the last log entry whoever was
  connected, so a restart longer than a turn aborted the game or made the player due lose at the first startup
  tick, and games idle at deploy time ended at once. Now:
  - `markEveryoneAway` sets `game.resumedAt` on every game in progress, and the turn counts from
    `max(last entry, resumedAt)`. The downtime is unknown after a crash, so a restart starts the turn again.
  - A player due to move who comes back from offline gets the time they were offline during the turn back
    (`withTurnCredit`, stored as `game.turnCredit` for the current turn), at most `reconnectGraceMs` per turn, so
    dropping the connection again and again cannot hold a game up. Switching between the room page and another
    page never counts.
  - While a player due is offline, the limit is held at `start + limit + reconnectGraceMs`, so it never runs out
    on someone reconnecting. A player who stays away is left to the opponent's claim of the win.
  - Chess clocks are unchanged: they are the game's own and keep running from the stored times.
  - Tests: `roomPresence.test.js` (over a restart and reconnects), `roomComponent.presence.test.js` (restart and
    reconnect), `server.restart.integration.test.js` (Tic-Tac-Toe down for ten minutes with a player due).
- **Aborted games are not matches** (follow-up from #78): `keepFinishedGame` and `listFinishedGames` skip them,
  so the match outbox never gets one (`roomComponent.finished.test.js`).
- **Deployment impact:** a game in progress when this deploys gets a fresh turn at startup instead of being
  ended for time it sat idle before the deploy.
