Shaped on 2026-10-20 from the owner's request that the UI stay smooth with no added friction while "many features
are adding text and pop-ups". It builds on the categories and the hold of CC-208 and CC-163, and on the component
contract of [CC-314](CC-314.md). [CC-099](CC-099.md) (rewards one beat at a time) depends on it.

## Problem

A new guest wins their first game on a phone. Within about a second the result, a level-up, an achievement and a
quest each want the screen, and the save-progress card waits below (MATCH_EXPERIENCE A9: up to five toasts over
about 85 % of the screen). CC-163 now holds lower toasts for 1.2 s while the result plays, which helps that one
moment. But each new feature still decides for itself whether to show a toast, a banner, a card or a dialog: the
site-wide request popup and the inbox (CC-207 and CC-139, PR #152), feedback (CC-290), save progress (CC-097),
streaks, daily share, and soon friends (CC-052) and reports (CC-044). Each is reasonable alone. Nothing counts
them together: not per screen, not per game, not per visit. Nothing keeps a quiet period while the player is
thinking about a move, apart from CC-207's own rule for its own popup. The friction this adds is real but unmeasured
until CC-313 gives us the numbers.

## Job to be done

When I am playing, I want nothing to pull my eyes off the board unless my opponent needs an answer or the game
can't go on, so I can play well. When the game ends, I want the good news one piece at a time and then a clear next
step, so it feels like a reward and not a pile of pop-ups.

## Appetite

M (up to 6 agent-hours). **Cut line**, dropped first in this order: the per-visit "ask" cap (keep per screen and per
game); the collapsing of social toasts into one line; the console counts. The arbiter, the quiet period during play
and the per-screen limits are never cut.

## Proposed solution

### One arbiter, on the client, extending what exists

`apps/web/src/lib/notify/` gains a pure `noticePolicy.js`, and `ToastProvider` becomes `NoticeCentre` (same
context, `useToast` and `useHold` keep working). Every toast, banner, card prompt and app-opened dialog asks the
centre before it shows, with `{ category, kind, key, surface }`. The centre answers **show**, **wait** (with a
reason) or **fold** (into the result summary, the inbox, or a collapsed line), and keeps the counts. It is a pure
reducer with injected time, like CC-099's reward queue, so it tests with fake timers.

Categories stay CC-208's six (request, turn, result, reward, social, system). This adds one: **ask**, for anything
that asks the player to do something we want (save progress, share, give feedback, turn on push later), priority 0,
lowest of all.

### The budget (numbers are open decision D2)

| Scope                                                        | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Per screen**                                               | At most 1 dialog, 1 banner, 1 toast visible on a phone (2 on desktop), as the CC-314 contract says. A higher priority replaces a lower banner; the lower one returns when it is still true                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **During play** (first move to the result, the quiet period) | Only three may break in: the turn category on the board itself, a **request from the opponent in this room** (draw offer, takeback), and **system** (connection lost). Everything else waits: rewards, social, site-wide requests and challenges (CC-207 already waits them, now the centre does it for all), asks. No dialog opened by the app. Tab title alerts are the only exception for a hidden tab |
| **Per game, at the result**                                  | The result moment (CC-163), then CC-099's beats in the result card. No reward toast on the room page while the result card shows (ADR-050 point 1). At most 3 sounds (ADR-050)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Per visit** (page load to tab close, kept in memory)       | At most 1 ask. Save progress keeps its own cap of 3 in total (PRODUCT_VISION §6.9). Social toasts after the third in a minute fold into one line ("3 players joined")                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Between games**                                            | A quiet gap of 2 s after "Rematch" or "New opponent" before any waiting toast shows, so the new board is seen first                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

What waits and what folds:

| Kind                                   | Over budget or in the quiet period                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reward (XP, level, achievement, quest) | Waits for the result card; if the player left, one toast later, or the profile                     |
| Social (joined, away, back)            | Shown in the presence row (the board's own place), toast only off the room page, collapsed after 3 |
| Site-wide request, challenge           | Waits until the game ends (CC-207's rule, now general), then the banner; after 60 s to the inbox   |
| Ask                                    | Dropped for this visit (never queued: a queued ask is nagging)                                     |
| System                                 | Never waits                                                                                        |

### CC-099 depends on CC-315 (decision D1)

CC-099's reward queue becomes the centre's **result surface**: the centre routes reward-category notices into it
while the result card is mounted, and CC-099 decides the order and timing of the beats inside the card. CC-099
then does not build its own toast routing (its "where each item sits", toasts point), which would otherwise be a
second routing system that this ticket would have to rewrite. CC-099 keeps everything else in its plan.

### Visible to the owner

The console's overview gets one table, from CC-312-style anonymous counts: notices shown, waited and folded per
category per India day. No ids (ADR-034). It answers "is the budget too tight or too loose".

## Non-goals

- No push notifications, email or inbox design (CC-147, CC-148, CC-215); ADR-031 stays the rule for those.
- No reward order or timing (CC-099), no outcome words (CC-163).
- No new component types: the contract is CC-314's.
- No server-side notification store. Per-visit counts live in memory.
- No per-player personalisation of the budget.

## Rabbit holes

- **A rule engine.** The policy is a table and a reducer of about 200 lines, not configuration. New features add
  a row.
- **Migrating every caller in one go.** Callers of `useToast` keep working (the centre wraps them with their
  category). Only the banners and the ask prompts change their call, about ten sites.
- **Cross-tab budgets.** Out: each tab has its own visit. Two tabs is rare and both stay quiet during play.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given a game in progress (first move made, no result), when a reward, social, site-wide request,
  challenge or ask notice arrives, then nothing shows and the notice waits or folds as the table says, while an
  opponent's request in this room and a lost connection still show at once. Test:
  `apps/web/src/lib/notify/noticePolicy.test.js` › "quiet during play: only the opponent's request and the
  connection break in".
- **AC-2** Given a phone viewport, when three toasts are asked for at once, then one is visible and the others wait
  their turn by priority; on desktop two. Test: `noticePolicy.test.js` › "one toast on a phone, two on desktop",
  and `e2e/notices.spec.js` › "never more than one toast on a phone".
- **AC-3** Given a banner on screen, when a higher priority banner arrives, then it replaces the lower one, and the
  lower one returns when it is still true; never two banners. Test: `noticePolicy.test.js` › "one banner, highest
  priority wins".
- **AC-4** Given a visit that has shown one ask, when a second ask is asked for, then it is dropped and never shown
  later in the visit. Test: `noticePolicy.test.js` › "one ask per visit, the rest dropped".
- **AC-5** Given the result card is mounted, when reward notices arrive, then they go to the result surface
  (CC-099), never to the toast region; after the card unmounts they toast again. Test:
  `NoticeCentre.test.jsx` › "rewards go to the result surface while it is mounted".
- **AC-6** Given "Rematch" pressed, when waiting toasts exist, then none shows for 2 s after the new board appears.
  Test: `noticePolicy.test.js` › "a quiet gap between games".
- **AC-7** Given every caller of `useToast`, `useHold` and the banner components today, when the centre is in, then
  their tests still pass unchanged apart from the category they pass. Test: the existing `ProgressToasts`,
  `GamificationToasts`, `RequestBanner`, `SiteRequests`, `ConnectionBanner` and `ResultCelebration` tests.
- **AC-8** Given a new guest's first win against a computer character on a 390 × 844 phone, when recorded from
  the last move to 10 s after, then at most one toast, one banner and no dialog are ever visible together, and
  Rematch is never covered. Test: `e2e/notices.spec.js` › "a first win stays within the budget".
- **AC-9** Given the console, when an admin opens the overview, then shown, waited and folded counts per category per
  day appear with `"<5"` for small numbers, and the event carries no id. Test: the counts integration test ›
  "notice counts store no id and hide small numbers".
- **AC-10** Given the flag off, when anything is shown, then behaviour is today's. Test: `flagPaths("notice_centre")`
  suite runs AC-1 to AC-6 with the flag on and the current tests with it off.

## Instrumentation

One event per notice decision, as an anonymous count per India day: `notice` with `category` (one of the seven),
`outcome` (`shown`, `waited`, `folded`, `dropped`) and `during_play` (true or false). No ids, no text, no URL
(ADR-034). Counted through the CC-312 counts store or the CC-173 analytics, whichever is live; the builder uses the
one on `main`.

## Threat model

1. **What are we building?** A client-side policy over notices, and an anonymous daily count.
2. **What can go wrong?** A safety notice is held back (a report confirmation, a block, connection loss), or a
   request is swallowed so the opponent waits forever.
3. **What are we doing about it?** System never waits. Requests wait only for the player's own game and keep their
   server-side expiry (CC-207), so the sender sees "no answer" honestly. The policy is pure and tested for every
   category.
4. **Good enough?** Yes: no new endpoint with data, nothing the server trusts from the client.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** counts of notice decisions per category per day. No player, no message.
- **Retention:** the counts store's (90 days in CC-312).
- **Who sees it:** the owner's console only.
- **Minors (ADR-028):** the same budget for everyone. A minor gets fewer notices already (no social prompts, ADR-028);
  nothing here adds any.
- **Defaults:** the budget is on for everyone once the flag is full; players cannot loosen it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Leave each feature to its own rules** (today): every feature is reasonable and the sum is not.
- **Server-decided notices**: the server cannot see what is on screen; the client can.
- **A notification inbox for everything** (CC-147): right for what can wait days, too slow for "your opponent offers
  a draw".

## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

Read on 2026-10-20 on `main` (a5a801c8). `lib/notify/categories.js` (CC-208) gives six categories a place, a
priority (request 4, turn 2, result 3, reward 1, social 2, system 5) and a rate limit. `ToastProvider` shows a toast
for 4 s (8 s with an action), skips the same message inside the rate limit, and while a higher category holds
(`useHold`, from `RequestBanner`, `SiteRequests` and `ResultCelebration`) keeps lower toasts waiting. That is a
priority rule per toast, not a budget: three toasts of the same priority all show, banners are not counted, the
save-progress card and other asks are not notices at all, and nothing knows a game is in progress. PR #152 (CC-207)
adds its own "never interrupt a game on the room page" rule for its popups, which is the right rule in one place.

### 2. How others do it (sources read 2026-10-20 unless marked)

| Source                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | What it says                                                                                                                                                                                     | What we take                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| NN/g, [Indicators, validations and notifications](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/indicators-validations-notifications/) (Kim Flaherty, 2024-01-17)                                                                                                                                                               | Action-required notifications are urgent and intrusive; passive ones are easy to miss and wrong for what the user must know                                                                      | Only action-required notices (the opponent's request, connection) may interrupt play                 |
| NN/g, [Modal and nonmodal dialogs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/modal-nonmodal-dialog/) (Therese Fessenden, 2017-04-23)                                                                                                                                                                                        | Do not interrupt high-stakes flows; nonessential content in a modal erodes trust                                                                                                                 | A game is our high-stakes flow: no app-opened dialog during play                                     |
| Mark, Gudith and Klocke, "The cost of interrupted work: more speed and stress", CHI 2008 (general knowledge, high confidence; the PDF did not parse)                                                                                                                                                               | Interrupted people compensate by working faster, at the cost of more stress, frustration and effort                                                                                              | Interruptions during a move cost the player even when they cope; a quiet period during play          |
| Iqbal and Bailey, interruptions at task breakpoints (CHI 2006 and later work; general knowledge, medium confidence)                                                                                                                                                                                                | Notices delivered at natural breakpoints between tasks cost less than in the middle of one                                                                                                       | Hold notices to the breakpoint: the result, and 2 s after a new board                                |
| Material Design 3, snackbar (m3.material.io; the page did not render for the fetch, general knowledge, high confidence)                                                                                                                                                                                            | One snackbar at a time; consecutive ones queue; a snackbar should not be the only way to an important action                                                                                     | One toast on a phone; queue by priority; no only-in-a-toast actions                                  |
| Apple HIG, Notifications and alerts (developer.apple.com; the pages did not render for the fetch, general knowledge, high confidence), and iOS 15 interruption levels (passive, active, time sensitive, critical)                                                                                                  | Use alerts sparingly; group notifications; only time-sensitive or critical ones break through Focus                                                                                              | Our categories as interruption levels: only system and the opponent's request break the quiet period |
| Android [notification channels](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/notifications/channels) (in CC-099)                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Importance per channel; the user has the last word                                                                                                                                               | Categories keep their per-category sound switch (CC-208)                                             |
| WCAG 2.2: [2.2.4 Interruptions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/interruptions.html) (AAA), [4.1.3 Status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html) (AA), [2.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-not-obscured-minimum.html) (AA, read 2026-10-20) | Interruptions can be postponed or suppressed by the user, except emergencies; status messages are announced without moving focus; content that appears on its own must not hide the focused item | The quiet period is 2.2.4 by default; one polite live region; toasts never over the focus            |
| CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023, "nagging" (India; in CC-099, general knowledge, high confidence)                                                                                                                                                                             | Repeated requests to do something the user did not ask for                                                                                                                                       | One ask per visit, dropped not queued                                                                |
| Our ADR-031                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | At most one unasked contact a day across channels, none while the tab is open                                                                                                                    | The same spirit inside the page                                                                      |

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

A game is the thing players came for, and our games are short (about 3 minutes, PRODUCT_VISION principle 8), so the
natural breakpoints are frequent: the result and the new board. Holding everything but the opponent and the
connection to those breakpoints costs a player at most a few minutes of delay and buys a calm board. The budget is
a table in code with tests, so CC-316's review can change a number in one line when CC-313's friction data says so.
