The owner said the site feels like it has "a lot of white space" and asked for effects, animations, images or text
to fill it. Production screenshots were taken on 2026-10-18 at 1280 × 720 and 390 × 844 (home, leaderboards, the
RPS and chess lobbies, profile).

### 2. How others do it (sources read 2026-10-18)

- **NN/g, [Designing empty states](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/empty-state-interface-design/) (2021-09-19):**
  never leave a container blank. Say what the state is (loading, failed or truly empty), teach what the feature
  does, and give a direct next step (a button).
- **Duolingo and chess.com** (general knowledge, medium confidence): the mascot fills empty and waiting states with
  one line and one button. Chess.com's profile and leaderboards for a new player show what you will see once you
  have played, and a "Play" button.
- **Poki and CrazyGames** (general knowledge, medium confidence): grids reflow to the screen width so a row never
  ends in a hole, and game pages carry a short "how to play" block under the game.
- **Lichess** (general knowledge, medium confidence): live counts and "recent games" only from real data. ADR-029
  forbids fake activity and "0 playing now" before a gate opens, so we do the same.
- **NN/g, [Animation duration](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/) (2020-02-09)** and ADR-016:
  short motion, and none of it may delay the first action. On low-end phones, CSS and SVG are enough. No video and
  no canvas backgrounds (ADR-016 amendment).

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

Fill space with things that help: a next step, a rule, a mascot line. Never filler, never fake numbers. Fix the
layout first, because two of the five holes are CSS defaults, not missing content.

## Why

Empty areas make a new site look unfinished and empty of people, which is exactly what a first-time visitor on a
budget phone should not feel (PRODUCT_VISION principles 2 and 10).

## Scope

- In: the per-screen fixes below. CSS, existing SVG art, short copy, and Bit once CC-153a exists (a simple
  placeholder from existing art until then).
- Out: live activity strips before their ADR-029 gates open, video, new dependencies.

Rules for every fill: one line of text, one button. Real data only (no "N playing now" or "recent wins" before
their ADR-029 gates). SVG and CSS, `transform` and `opacity` only, reduced motion respected, no video, and no new
dependency.

## Verification

Built on `cc-259-dead-space` in five commits, one per screen (owner decisions in ADR-050: a placeholder from
existing art, Log in and Sign up both in a phone menu, no "More games" tile yet).

1. Home grid `auto-fit` and lobby cards `align-items: start`.
2. Visitor profile: a greyed-out preview of the card (level 1, an unlit streak flame, the first three achievements
   of the catalog, locked) with Play as a guest and Log in.
3. Leaderboards with fewer than 25 entries (the ADR-029 threshold, until CC-228): a "How to get on the board" panel
   beside the board at 64 rem and wider, under it on phones. Rules only, no numbers.
4. Lobby "How to play" strip: three steps per game from a `howToPlay` entry in the client game registry.
5. Phone header (40 rem and narrower): one row with the square glyph, the navigation and a Menu disclosure button
   (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`). It holds sound, volume, level, name, Log in, Sign up and Log out, opens
   inside the header (it pushes the page down, so a tap meant for the page never lands on it), moves the focus to
   its first control, and closes on Escape (focus back on the button), on a click outside and on another page.

Tests: unit tests beside each piece (`VisitorProfile.test.jsx`, `LeaderboardPage.test.jsx`, `HowToPlay.test.jsx`,
`HeaderMenu.test.jsx`). `e2e/dead-space.spec.js` measures each screen at 1280 x 720 (desktop project) and at
390 x 844 and 360 x 800 (mobile project): grid tracks against items, card heights against content, content width,
and the phone header at most 64 px tall. The existing browser tests open the menu through the `header(page)` helper
in `e2e/fixtures.js` before they use Log in, Sign up, Log out, sound or the level chip.

PR #90 review fixes: the phone navigation has a zero flex basis, so three or four links (an account holder, an admin)
shrink and scroll instead of wrapping the Menu button onto a second row, checked at 360 px. The live region that
says who you are sits outside the menu, so a closed menu no longer silences it. A guest with XP gets a dot on the
Menu button so the Save progress nudge inside is still seen.

Not done here: visual baselines (CC-155 is not built yet) and the BACKLOG, PROGRESS and KNOWN_ISSUES updates.
