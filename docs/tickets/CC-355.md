Shaped with [CC-354](CC-354.md) and [CC-356](CC-356.md) after the owner lifted the feature freeze for these three
on 2026-10-22 (ADR-082). We take the general pattern (the next round is right there, the score keeps running), never
another game's theme, art or words.

## Problem

Ravi beats Pip at Tic Tac Toe on his phone. The result card says he won, and under it are three buttons: "Rematch",
"New opponent" and "Leave room". "Rematch" is a word he has to think about, and after pressing it he waits a moment
while the computer "accepts". The score of the evening ("You 2 – 1 Pip") appears only from the second game, only on
the result card, and is gone while he plays. With a friend the same button sends a request, which is right, but the
words do not say so. Nothing makes the next game feel one tap away, and nothing keeps the running score in view.

## Job to be done

When a game ends and I want another, I want the next one to start with one tap (or by itself, if I'm playing the
computer and leave it), with the running score always in view, so a short session flows game to game and I can stop
whenever I like.

## Appetite

M (up to 6 agent-hours), one PR, web only. **Cut line**, dropped first in this order: the "at most 3 in a row"
counter (the countdown then never chains, it shows only after a game the player started by hand); the series line
during play on phones (it stays on the result card); the Hinglish copy file's tests beyond the C9 check. Never cut:
Play again, the countdown's stop rules, never auto-starting with a person, Leave in one press, both flags with both
paths.

## Proposed solution

### What the player sees after a game

The action bar inside the result card (CC-157) keeps its three places. The first one, today's `RematchPanel`,
becomes **Play again**, with the same server request underneath (ADR-015.7, CC-159): nothing changes on the server.

1. **Against a computer character, Rock Paper Scissors or Tic Tac Toe, adult account** (flag
   `next_round_countdown`, audience `adults`, so the server's age policy decides and unknown age is not adult):
   1.2 s after the result moment (CC-163) a line under Play again counts down 5 seconds, then the client presses
   Play again itself (the same `ask rematch` intent; the character accepts as today, `botComponent.acceptRematch`).
2. **The countdown stops, for this room, at once and for good** on: Stop timer; any press, tap or key anywhere on
   the page (Play again starts at once; Leave room leaves); the tab becoming hidden (`visibilitychange`); a request
   banner or any CC-315 hold of the request category; the connection reconnecting; reduced motion does not stop it
   (it is text, not motion). It never starts after a game in which the player made no move, after an aborted game,
   or after 3 games in a row that a countdown started (counted in memory per room, reset by any game started by
   hand).
3. **Against a computer, everyone else (guests, minors, unknown ages) and every chess game:** Play again only, no
   countdown (ICO children's code standard 5 names auto-play; ADR-028.1 unknown age means minor; a chess game is too
   long to roll into the next one by itself).
4. **Against a person:** never automatic. Play again sends the request; the panel says who we wait for and until
   when (C5). When both press Play again the next game starts, as today. The other player's "wants to play again"
   line and Play again accept it. Declines and no answer read as today, in the new words.
5. **Leave room** stays one press after a game (no confirm), and New opponent stays as it is.

### The running score

Behind `next_round`, the match bar's score line (MatchFrame's `score` slot) shows the room's series from game 1:
"Game 3 · You 2 – 0 Rocky" (wins only, draws not in the numbers, as CC-161 recommends), during play and at the
result. A game's own score (Rock Paper Scissors rounds) stays the second part of the line: "Game 3 · You 2 – 0
Rocky · Round 2 of 3". The `SeriesScore` paragraph in `RematchPanel` is removed (C12: one place for one result). This
is a minimal line from `room.series`, which the room view already sends; CC-161 later replaces it with its
scoreboard (history dots, animation, server history) in the same slot.

## Non-goals

- Any server change: rematch, its window, its cooldown, the bot's accept and the series stay as they are.
- CC-161's scoreboard (history dots, animation, series history on the server) and CC-172's layout.
- Auto-start against a person in any form, a "ready" lobby, or a countdown for chess.
- A setting to turn the countdown off for good (Stop timer covers a room; a setting is a follow-up if asked).

## Rabbit holes

- **Timers and the result moment.** The countdown is one hook with injected time (`useNextRoundCountdown`), tested
  with fake timers; it starts from the finished view's arrival, never from a render.
- **Fighting CC-099.** The countdown lives under Play again, which CC-099 requires never to move; it does not mount
  an effect or a sound, and it does not register a notice.
- **Two tabs.** Each tab's countdown is its own; the first ask wins on the server and the second tab sees the new
  game. Tested with two clients on the bot path.

## Acceptance criteria

Each runs in `flagPaths("next_round", …)` and, for the countdown, `flagPaths("next_round_countdown", …)`.

- **AC-1** Given an adult (flag `next_round_countdown` on) who just beat Pip at Tic Tac Toe after making moves, when
  1.2 s pass after the result, then "The next game against Pip starts in 5 seconds." shows, counts down each second,
  and at 0 the client sends one `ask rematch`. Test: `useNextRoundCountdown.test.jsx` › "starts 1.2 s after the
  result and asks once at zero"
- **AC-2** Given the countdown running, when Stop timer, any press or key, the tab hidden, a request banner, or a
  reconnect happens, then it stops for this room and nothing is sent. Test: `useNextRoundCountdown.test.jsx` ›
  "each stop rule ends it for good"
- **AC-3** Given 3 games in a row started by the countdown, when the third ends, then no countdown shows; after a game
  started by hand it may show again. Test: `useNextRoundCountdown.test.jsx` › "at most three in a row"
- **AC-4** Given a guest, a minor, an account of unknown age (flag `next_round_countdown` off for them), or any chess
  game, when a game against a computer ends, then only Play again shows, with "Play another game against {name}.".
  Test: `NextRound.test.jsx` › "no countdown for guests, minors, unknown ages or chess"
- **AC-5** Given a game against a person, when it ends, then no countdown ever shows and no request is sent until the
  player presses Play again; the next game starts only when both have pressed it. Test: `NextRound.test.jsx` ›
  "never automatic with a person" and `e2e/rematch.spec.js` › "both press Play again to start the next game"
- **AC-6** Given a finished game, when the player presses Leave room, then they leave in one press with no dialog.
  Test: `MatchShell.test.jsx` › "Leave room after a game is one press"
- **AC-7** Given a room in game 1 against Rocky, when the board shows, then the match bar reads "Game 1 · You 0 – 0
  Rocky", and after a win and a draw "Game 3 · You 1 – 0 Rocky"; the result card has no second score. Test:
  `matchShell.test.js` › "series line from game 1, wins only" and `MatchShell.test.jsx` › "one score on screen"
- **AC-8** Given the flag `next_round` off, when a game ends, then the panel and words are exactly as today. Test:
  the off side of every suite above (`flagPaths`)
- **AC-9** Every English line has a Hinglish twin, "tum" where it speaks to the player, never "aap"; no visible
  "rematch". Test: `nextRoundCopy.test.js` › "every line has a tum twin and no jargon"
- **AC-10** Given an adult against Rocky on a phone (390 × 844), when the countdown reaches 0, then game 2 starts,
  the match bar says "Game 2", and Play again's box never moved during the countdown. Test: `e2e/nextRound.spec.js`
  › "the countdown starts the next game against Rocky"

## Instrumentation

Anonymous counts only (ADR-034). Flag exposure and success counts (ADR-073.6): `next_round` success "a next game
started from the result within 30 s", `next_round_countdown` success "a next game the countdown started had a
first move". Sent with `api.sendFlagSuccess`. The existing press counts (CC-174) already name the controls
(`room.rematch.ask`, new `room.next_round.stop`). The two guardrail ratios come from these counts and the existing
aborted-game count; no new event.

## Threat model

Web only, on top of the existing rematch request. What can go wrong: a client that auto-asks forever (the server's
rematch cooldown, the bot's limit of games at once, the per-player event budget and ADR-021's hourly cap bound it);
a countdown that starts a game with a person (only when every opponent seat is a computer, checked from the room
view, and the server still needs the person's yes); a countdown for a minor (the flag's audience is decided on the
server from the age policy, the browser never works out an age). Good enough: the worst case is an unwanted game
against a computer, ended with one press, which records nothing if no move is made.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** nothing new is stored or sent. The "three in a row" counter is in memory per room and tab.
- **Retention:** none.
- **Who sees it:** the player sees their room's series, which the room view already sends to everyone in the room.
- **Minors (ADR-028):** no countdown for minors, guests or unknown ages (the `adults` audience); they get the button.
- **Defaults:** both flags off; the countdown is stoppable with one press and never chains past 3.

## Alternatives considered

- **Countdown for everyone, including guests.** Rejected: ADR-028.1 treats guests as minors, and ICO standard 5 names
  auto-play.
- **A server-side "next game starts in 5 s" state.** Rejected: a server timer per room for a client convenience, and
  a person could never be part of it anyway.
- **Keep "Rematch".** Rejected: C7 lists "rematch request" as jargon; "Play again" says what happens.
- **Wait for CC-161 for the score.** Rejected: the brief asks for the score always in view; one text line from data
  the view already has costs little, and CC-161 replaces it.

## Research (written before any code)

### 1. How our code does it today

- `RematchPanel.jsx` (CC-037, CC-104, CC-159, CC-162) is the first item of the result's action bar
  (`actionBarItems`: rematch, new opponent, leave). "Rematch" sends `ask rematch`; against a computer the
  character accepts after its think delay (`botComponent.acceptRematch`, `botTurns.js`), against a person it is a
  request with a 1 minute life and a 10 s re-ask cooldown (`roomLifetimes.js`); both players pressing starts the
  game. The rematch window is 30 minutes.
- The series (`room.series { games, wins }`) is kept by the room (`nextSeries` in `roomComponent.js`) and shown only
  by `SeriesScore`, from game 2, on the result card. The match bar has a score slot used only for a game's own score
  (`inGameScore`, Rock Paper Scissors rounds).
- Leave room after a game is one press already (the confirm is only while a game is on).
- CC-161 (Todo, research done) will add a scoreboard with history dots; it recommends showing the score from game 1
  and counting wins only.

### 2. How other products do it (read 2026-10-22)

- **Lichess:** the result offers "Rematch" and "New opponent"; a rematch offer to a person waits for their yes, and
  shows "Rematch offer sent". Source: lila's translation source,
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lichess-org/lila/master/translation/source/site.xml (keys `rematch`,
  `rematchOfferSent`, `newOpponent`), and https://github.com/lichess-org/lila. We take: never auto-start with a
  person, and the two clear choices.
- **Netflix "Autoplay next episode":** the next episode plays by itself, and it is a per-profile switch: "Toggle the
  switch next to Autoplay Next Episode". Source: https://help.netflix.com/en/node/121518. We take: the next one
  starts by itself only when nothing else needs a decision, and it is easy to stop.
- **ICO Age appropriate design code, standard 5:** names "auto-play features which encourage users to continue
  playing" among sticky features, and asks to "present options to continue playing … neutrally" and allow a break
  "at any time". Source:
  https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/age-appropriate-design-a-code-of-practice-for-online-services/5-detrimental-use-of-data/.
  We take: no countdown for anyone not surely an adult.

### 3. What is best for ClassiCade

Our games are short and our players are often alone with a computer character, so the next round should be one tap
away and, for adults, may start by itself for a few seconds, the way the next episode does. A person is never pulled
into a game. The countdown stops on any sign of the player, never chains forever, and never runs for guests or
minors. The running score is the cheapest strong pull ("one more to make it 3 – 1"), so it moves into the match bar
from game 1, using data the room already sends, and CC-161 later makes it richer.
