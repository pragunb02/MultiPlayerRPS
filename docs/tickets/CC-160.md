## Why

The request popup sat inside the stage above the board, the asker's side was a separate card, there was no
countdown, and how a request ended was a toast that the player could miss (MATCH_EXPERIENCE A2, A3, A7).

## Scope

- In: `components/RequestBanner`, the first thing in the room under the site header. Incoming: the question as an
  assertive announcement, Accept, Decline and Hide (Escape inside it hides it too), a `<progress>` countdown with the
  minutes and seconds. Outgoing: what the asker waits for, the countdown and Cancel, and where the opponent is
  (CC-207). Answers ("Bob declined your draw offer", "Bob accepted. Starting the next game", "No answer from Bob")
  shown to both sides for 4 s. The request sound comes from `roomSounds`. Focus never moves. While a request is open,
  reward toasts wait (CC-208). It replaces `pages/RoomPage/RoomRequests.jsx`.
- Out: emotes and the match bar (CC-157, CC-169).

## Design

- The banner is sticky at the top of the window while the page scrolls, and in its resting place it is above the
  room's header and board, so it never covers a square without scrolling (checked at 390×844 and 1280×720 by
  `match-shell.spec.js`).
- The countdown is a `<progress>` element plus text inside a `data-cc-ticker` wrapper, so the CSP needs no inline
  style and the every-press check does not count its ticking.
- `useRequestAnswers` compares each RoomView with the one before, so a reload is not news. A rematch nobody answered
  stays with its choices until the asker closes it.
- Offer draw and Rematch say why they wait after a "no" ("You can ask again in a moment") or once the draw offers are
  used up, from the view's `askAgainAt` and `requestsLeft` (`games/useAskReason.js`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Sticky at the top, never over the board, visible without scrolling on 390×844.
- [x] Question, Accept and Decline, asker waiting with Cancel, `<progress>` countdown, request sound, assertive
      announcement, no focus steal.
- [x] Answers shown to both sides for 4 s.
- [x] Unit tests (`RoomPage.alerts.test.jsx`, `roomUpdates.test.js`), browser tests (`requests.spec.js`, every state
      in `match-shell.spec.js`).
