## Problem

Level, XP, streaks, quests and achievements are the reasons a player comes back (ADR-013), but each screen draws
them its own way: a `<progress>` in the header chip, `XpPop` floating text on the result, a `StreakCard` on the
profile, flat medal tiles in `AchievementGrid`, quest bars that are plain lines, and numbers that jump with no sense
of gain. There is no shared look for "progress" in a game that sells itself as an arcade, and CC-099's reward beats
need these pieces to play one at a time.

## Job to be done

When I gain XP, keep a streak or finish a quest, I want to see it fill, count up and light up in the same arcade
style everywhere, so my progress feels real and I know how close the next level or badge is.

## Appetite

Part A: M (up to 6 agent-hours), new files only plus the design page. Part B: M, the placements. **Cut line**, part
A: the progress ring's tick marks; part B: the home "Your arcade" strip's next-badge tile. The five components, the
counter budget and their accessibility are never cut.

## Proposed solution

Five components in `apps/web/src/components/Gaming/`, each a contract entry with every state, drawn only from
tokens (CC-329's gold scale and medal tokens, the player and brand roles), animated in the tiers ADR-075 assigns.

### The components

| Component          | What it shows                                                                       | API                                                                                                             | States                                                                              | Motion (catalogue id, tier)                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `LevelBadge`       | The level number in the arcade hexagon (the drawing in `art/badges/LevelBadge.jsx`) | `level`, `size` (`sm` 24, `md` 40, `lg` 72), `levelUp` (plays the pop once)                                     | rest, just levelled up                                                              | M-330-1 pop: scale 0.6 → 1.08 → 1, 360 ms `--ease-spring`, simple tier (CSS)                                      |
| `XpBar`            | XP inside the current level: a bar and "120 / 300 XP"                               | `current`, `needed`, `gained` (the part just earned, drawn brighter), `size` (`thin` 4 px, `md` 10 px), `label` | empty, partway, full (level up next), gaining                                       | M-330-2 fill: `scaleX` of the fill, up to 600 ms `--ease-out`, intermediate tier (Motion `progress` preset)       |
| `StreakFlame`      | The flame and "3 day streak"                                                        | `days`, `best`, `today` (played today or not), `size`                                                           | none (0 days: grey flame, "Play today to start a streak"), lit, lit today, new best | M-330-3 flicker: 3 flickers on `transform`, 900 ms total, only when `days` grows, simple tier                     |
| `ProgressRing`     | One quest or goal as a ring with its number inside: "2/3"                           | `value`, `max`, `label` (what it counts, required), `tone` (`you`, `gold`, `xp`), `size`                        | not started, partway, done (a check and gold ring)                                  | M-330-4 sweep: `stroke-dashoffset` from script, 600 ms, the ADR-012 stroke-draw exception, simple tier            |
| `AchievementBadge` | One achievement as a medal by tier with its icon                                    | `achievement` (id, name, tier, unlockedAt), `locked` (silhouette with "how to get it"), `size`, `fresh`         | locked, unlocked, fresh (just earned)                                               | M-330-5 pop-in: scale and opacity, 360 ms, simple tier; the burst around it is CC-328's `achievement`             |
| `AnimatedNumber`   | A counter for XP, wins and coins                                                    | `value`, `from` (optional), `format`                                                                            | static, counting                                                                    | M-330-6 count: Motion `animate()` on a `MotionValue`, at most 1200 ms (`--duration-celebrate`), intermediate tier |

Rules for all of them:

1. **The number is in the DOM from the first render.** `AnimatedNumber` renders the final value in a visually
   hidden span and the counting digits `aria-hidden`; a screen reader reads the final value once, from the
   sentence around it (EFFECTS-STACK section 6.2). Under reduced motion the final value shows at once.
2. **Motion only where it is already loaded.** `AnimatedNumber` and `XpBar` import Motion's `animate` lazily the
   first time a value grows; until it arrives (and on the first route, where Motion is never loaded) the new value
   simply appears. So the header can use them without breaking the bundle gate.
3. **Bars are `role="progressbar"`** with `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuetext`
   ("120 of 300 XP to level 4"); the fill moves by `transform: scaleX()` set from script (allowed by the CSP, ADR-016
   point 4), never `width`, never a `style` attribute in markup.
4. **Colour carries meaning, with words.** XP is `--color-xp` with the gradient `--gradient-xp`; your progress in
   the result is `--color-you`; gold (`--color-gold-bright` fill, ink text) is only for a completed goal or a
   gold-tier medal; a locked badge is `--color-locked` with "Win 2 more games" in words.
5. **One standout effect.** A component never runs a canvas effect itself; the burst around a fresh badge or a
   level-up is `play("achievement")` or `play("level_up")`, called by CC-099's beat, and the host plays one at a time.
6. **Never pressure.** No countdowns, no "at risk", no "only 3 % have this" (ADR-050 point 2, PRODUCT_VISION §6.9).
   A streak that broke shows "Best: 7 days" and "Play today to start again", never a sad flame.

## Non-goals

- New rewards or rules (ADR-013, CC-143); coins (CC-142).
- The beat order and timing (CC-099); the trophy shelves (CC-153 part e).
- Canvas effects (CC-328).
- Avatars and frames (CC-153 part f).

## Rabbit holes

- **Counting in the header on the first route.** Motion is lazy; the number appears without counting there until
  Motion is cached.
- **A ring as a library.** One SVG circle with `stroke-dasharray`; no chart library.
- **Ten sizes.** Three sizes per component, from the space tokens.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given each component in each state, when rendered, then it draws that state from tokens only and a test
  names each state. Test: `components/Gaming/Gaming.test.jsx` › "every component in every state"
- **AC-2** Given an `XpBar` and a `ProgressRing`, when rendered, then each is a progressbar (or labelled image for
  the ring) with its value and a value text in words. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` › "progress is read in words"
- **AC-3** Given `AnimatedNumber` going from 120 to 150, when it renders, then 150 is in the accessible text from the
  first render, and the visible digits reach 150 within 1200 ms. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` › "the final number is
  there from the start"
- **AC-4** Given reduced motion, when any component's value grows, then the final state shows at once and no
  animation or Motion import starts. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` › "reduced motion shows the final state"
- **AC-5** Given a locked badge and a broken streak, when rendered, then each says in words how to get it or start
  again, and no text says "at risk", "only" or a countdown. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` › "no pressure copy"
- **AC-6** Given part B with the `gaming_layer` flag on, when a guest opens home, the header and the profile, then
  each place shows the components in the table; with the flag off they look as before. Test:
  `pages/HomePage/HomePage.test.jsx` › "Your arcade strip follows its flag" (in `flagPaths`)
- **AC-7** Completion, tokens only: given the new CSS, when `npm run lint` runs, then it passes with no exception.
  Test: `npm run lint` (CI)
- **AC-8** Completion, every state: given `/admin/design`, when opened, then it shows every component in every state
  in the device's theme. Test: `pages/AdminPage/DesignPage.test.jsx` › "shows the gaming layer"
- **AC-9** Completion, layout probe: given part B's placements, when the probe runs at 320 to 1280 and 200 % zoom,
  then the header, home strip and profile never overflow and the XP text never clips. Test: `e2e/profiles.spec.js` ›
  "gaming layer passes the layout probe"
- **AC-10** Completion, accessibility: given the design page and part B's pages, when axe runs, then 0 serious
  findings. Test: `e2e/owner.spec.js` › "axe: the design page shows the gaming layer", and for part B `e2e/profiles.spec.js` › "axe: gaming layer"
- **AC-11** Completion, motion: given catalogue entries M-330-1 to M-330-6, when each plays, then it uses its tier
  and duration, at most 30 Motion elements animate at once, and none is a standout effect. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` ›
  "durations and tiers match the catalogue"
- **AC-12** Completion, cleanup: given a counter or a fill animating, when the component unmounts, then its Motion
  animation is stopped and no timer is left. Test: `Gaming.test.jsx` › "unmount stops the count"
- **AC-13** Completion, bundle: given the build, when the bundle gate runs, then the first route stays at or under
  150 kB gzip and Motion is not on it. Test: `node scripts/bin/check-bundle.mjs` (CI step "Bundle size budget")

## Threat model

1. **What are we building?** Components over data players already see. 2. **What can go wrong?** A guest's or
   minor's level shows to others. 3. **What are we doing about it?** The components show only the viewer's own data;
   other players' levels stay where ADR-028 already allows. 4. **Good enough?** Yes.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** none new. **Retention:** unchanged. **Who sees it:** the player, their own progress. **Minors
  (ADR-028):** the same components; nothing shown to others changes. **Defaults:** part B off until ramped.

## Research (written before any code)

1. **How our code does it today.** `LevelChip` (a `<progress>` and "Lv 3"), `art/badges/LevelBadge.jsx` and
   `Medal.jsx`, `art/effects/XpPop.jsx` and `StreakFlame.jsx`, `components/Streaks/StreakCard.jsx`,
   `components/Quests/*` and `components/Achievements/AchievementGrid.jsx`, each with its own styles.
2. **How others do it.** Duolingo (blog.duolingo.com, 2022-01-21 and 2022-01-31): a streak flame and an XP bar that
   fill on the result, one moment at a time; Clash Royale and Brawl Stars (CC-099 sources): trophy roads and badges
   as shelves with silhouettes for locked ones. EFFECTS-STACK section 6.2: counters as a `MotionValue` with the
   final number in the DOM.
3. **What is best for ClassiCade.** One set of progress pieces, drawn in our arcade style, readable in words, that
   CC-099 can play one beat at a time and every page can reuse.
