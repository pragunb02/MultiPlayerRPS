## Problem

Below a strong hero, home drops to flat white cards with the game art in a box inside the card, a coloured strip
that reads as a glitch, a fourth button look ("Play" pill), and no response to hover on any card. On a phone the hero
is 1.5 screens tall, so no game shows. A returning guest gets a dark "You're playing as Guest-K5B6." pill that looks
like a button and is not one, and the Daily puzzle card is a wide box with one line. The profile is one scroll of
6757 px on a phone, with the trophy case, privacy, password, data download and account deletion mixed together, and
"Delete my account" looks exactly like "Download my data" (AUDIT-2026-10, Home and Profiles findings; items 9, 11).

## Job to be done

When I open ClassiCade I want to see the games at once and start one in one tap, and when I open my profile I want
my progress first and my settings in their own clear place, so the site feels like an arcade I belong to and never
like a form I might break.

## Appetite

M (up to 6 agent-hours), two PRs: home, then profile. **Cut line**, dropped first: the 768 px scroller variant (three
columns instead); the Settings tab's side navigation (a list of anchors at the top instead). The Play now button,
the art to the edges, the hover lift, the short phone hero, the three tabs and the danger zone are never cut.

## Proposed solution

Built only from CC-323's components (`Card` with `CardMedia`, `Button`, `PageHeader`, `Sections` and `Section`,
`Tabs`, `EmptyState`, `Skeleton`) and the owner's rules in CONTRACT.md section 3, "Pages, sections and data": titled
sections each with a heading and one line, colour only with meaning and a word, data with its unit and window,
aligned numbers, one rhythm.

### Home below the hero

1. **The hero, shorter on phones.** At 320 and 390 the hero is at most `60svh` (at 390 × 844 about 450 px): the
   headline in `--type-display` steps to `--type-title`, the lead to one line, the hero art beside the text at 30 %
   width, not below it. The first game card's top edge is at most 85 % down the first screen, so its art shows.
2. **A real Play now.** Visitor: "Play as a guest" (`accent`, as today). Guest or account: the hero's main action is
   **`Play <last game>`** (`accent`, `lg`), linking to the lobby of the game they finished most recently (the newest
   entry of their match history), or **`Play now`** to the Rock Paper Scissors lobby (the quickest game) when they
   have none. Under it one quiet line in `--type-label` muted: "Playing as Guest-K5B6" with the name cut by an
   ellipsis at the line's end. The pill is gone.
3. **Event greeting** (CC-328): the generic `EventGreeting` stays above the headline, wherever this redesign puts it.
4. **Game cards.** `Card variant="accent" tone=<game> interactive` with `CardMedia` running the game art to the
   card's edges (16:9, `--radius-lg` on the top corners only), `CardHeader` with the name and one line ("2 players ·
   about 1 minute"), and `CardLink` stretched over the card: one Tab stop named "Play Chess". Hover and focus: lift
   4 px with `--elevation-hover` (CC-323 D5, CSS only); press: back to 0. The "Play" pill and the bottom strip go.
   Grid: one column under 30rem, two from 30rem, **three from 48rem** (so 768 shows all three, no orphan Chess),
   three at 1024 and 1440 inside a 72rem container.
5. **Daily puzzle card.** A `Card variant="raised"` with art: a new drawing `art/illustrations/DailyPuzzleArt.jsx` (a
   corner of the night board from CC-329 with a gold crown and the puzzle number), the line "Black to move. Find 2
   moves.", the account or guest's puzzle streak in words when there is one ("Solved 3 days running"), and one
   `primary` "Solve" button. At 1024 and up it sits beside the Daily quests card in two equal columns.
6. **Daily quests card** (guests and accounts): the same height as the puzzle card, its quests as a list with a
   number and unit each ("Win 2 games · 1 of 2"), and "Open your player console" becomes `<Button as="link"
variant="secondary">` "Your console".
7. **A slot for CC-330.** Between the hero and the games, an empty `data-cc-slot="your-arcade"` region that CC-330
   part B fills behind its flag. Nothing renders in it in this ticket.

## Rabbit holes

- **"Last game played" as new data.** It is the newest entry of the player's match history (`useMatchHistory`),
  already loaded for the console; if it is still loading, the button says "Play now" and does not change once
  loaded (no layout shift, no label swap after first paint).
- **Tabs as client state.** Each tab is a route, so Back and shared links work; no hidden panels.
- **Moving every settings component.** They move as whole components into sections; their insides do not change.

   (ADR-048). 4. **Good enough?** Yes.

## Research (written before any code)

1. **How our code does it today.** `HomePage.jsx`: the hero with `HeroArt`, the guest pill
   ("You're playing as …"), a `ul` of game cards with `cardArt` boxes and a `play` pill, the player console and daily
   puzzle sections. `ProfilePage.jsx`: one page with stats, streaks, quests, achievements, avatar picker, privacy,
   blocked players, account settings, download and delete. Audit evidence: `home-visitor 390-dark`,
   `home-guest 1280-light`, `profile-account 390-light`.
2. **How others do it.** chess.com and lichess home pages put "Play" first with one tap to the last time control
   (general knowledge, high confidence); GitHub's repository settings end in a red-bordered "Danger Zone"
   (github.com, general knowledge, high confidence); NN/g on tabs (Laubheimer, "Tabs, Used Right", 2016): tabs for
   parallel content the user switches between. WCAG 1.4.10 for the 320 px reflow.
3. **What is best for ClassiCade.** Players come to play: the first screen must show games and a one-tap start.
   The profile is pride first (progress) and admin second (settings), with the one irreversible action set apart.
