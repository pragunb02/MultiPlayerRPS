## Problem

A player has played every day for 23 days. On day 24 she has an exam and does not open the app. Tomorrow the streak
card says 0 and "No streak yet". One busy day wiped out three weeks, and the honest reaction is to stop caring about
the streak at all. The board row and ADR-026.5 promise one free freeze a week, earned by playing, at most 2 held,
never a coin sink.

## Job to be done

When life gets in the way for a day, I want my streak to survive without me doing anything, so that the streak
rewards a habit rather than punishing a single missed day.

## Appetite

M (up to 6 agent-hours, shared with CC-146). **Cut line:** first the next milestone line, then the earn back, then
the frozen days in the guest rebuild (the holdings still move).

## Proposed solution

1. **Earn.** The first counted match of each week (Monday to Sunday in the player's own days, CC-146) gives one
   freeze. A player holds at most 2; a freeze earned while holding 2 is not kept.
2. **Cover, automatically.** When a counted match comes after missed days, and the player holds at least as many
   freezes as days missed, one freeze is spent per missed day and the streak carries on (+1 for today). If the
   freezes cannot cover every missed day, none is spent and the streak starts again at 1. Nothing is spent in the
   background: the read of the streak shows "a freeze is covering yesterday" and the next match makes it so.
3. **Earn back.** On the day the streak carries on after a freeze, a second counted match that day gives one freeze
   back (still at most 2), once per cover.
4. **Longest streak kept.** `best` never goes down. A frozen day keeps the streak alive but adds no day to it.
5. **Milestones** at 3, 7, 30, 100 and 365 days: the card says the next one. No reward, no pop-up (cut line).
6. **Only on the play streak.** Not the win streak, not the puzzle streak (ADR-061.6 keeps its own rules).
7. **UI.** A small indicator beside the streak: a snowflake and "1 of 2 freezes", in the CC-314 info tokens, with
   one plain line when something is happening (covering, earn back). CC-330 restyles it later.

## Non-goals

- Buying, gifting or trading freezes, or a paid "repair" (ADR-026.3, ADR-026.5).
- Freezes for the win streak or the puzzle streak.
- Reminders or notifications about a streak (CC-147, quiet hours for minors).
- Milestone rewards or badges.

## Rabbit holes

- **A background job to spend freezes at midnight.** Not needed: the rule is a pure function of the stored streak
  and the day, applied on read and at the next match.
- **Guest rebuilds** (ADR-013.8) replay the history without freezes. The frozen days are kept on the streak so the
  replay treats them as covered and a protected streak survives a sign-in.

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given no freeze this week, when the first counted match of the week ends, then the player holds one more,
  at most 2. Test: `freezes.test.js` › "earns one freeze with the first counted match of a week, at most 2"
- **AC-2** Given one missed day and one freeze, when the next match ends, then the freeze is spent and the streak
  carries on. Test: `freezes.test.js` › "covers a missed day with a freeze automatically"
- **AC-3** Given two missed days and one freeze, then no freeze is spent and the streak starts at 1. Test:
  `freezes.test.js` › "spends nothing when the freezes cannot cover every missed day"
- **AC-4** Given a freeze spent, when two counted matches end on the day the streak carries on, then one freeze comes
  back, once. Test: `freezes.test.js` › "earns a freeze back with two matches the day after"
- **AC-5** Given any sequence, then `best` never goes down and a frozen day adds nothing to `current`. Test:
  `freezes.test.js` › "keeps the longest streak and counts only days played"
- **AC-6** Given a missed yesterday and a freeze, when the streak is read today, then it shows as alive and covered
  and nothing is stored. Test: `freezes.test.js` › "shows a covered streak on read without spending anything"
- **AC-7** Given the day boundary in a summer-time zone and a zone change, then covering counts the player's own
  days. Test: `gamificationComponent.localDays.test.js` › "covers a missed New York day across the summer time change"
- **AC-8** Given an old stats document with no freeze fields, then it reads as 0 freezes and works. Test:
  `freezes.test.js` › "reads an old streak with no freeze fields"
- **AC-9** Given `streak_freezes` off, then freezes are neither earned nor spent and the view has none. Test:
  `gamificationComponent.localDays.test.js` › "with the streak_freezes flag off (CC-312)"
- **AC-10** Given a guest with freezes signing in, then the account holds the larger, at most 2, and a frozen day keeps
  the rebuilt streak. Test: `gamificationComponent.localDays.test.js` › "keeps a protected streak and the larger
  holding when a guest signs in"
- **AC-11** Given freezes in the view, the streak card shows the indicator and plain copy. Test:
  `gamification.test.jsx` › "shows the freezes beside the streak" and "uses no pressure words in the streak lines"
- **AC-12** Export lists the freezes and erase removes them (they live in the stats document). Test:
  `gamificationComponent.localDays.test.js` › "exports the freezes with the stats"

## Instrumentation

The flag's exposures and success (`flags.recordSuccess("streak_freezes", …)` when a freeze covers a day). Anonymous
counts only (ADR-034).

## Threat model

We add a small counter to the stats document. A player could try to farm freezes with guests (cap 2 and the larger
holding, never the sum), or replay a match to earn one twice (the exactly-once key window of ADR-013.2 already
covers it). Freezes have no value outside the streak and cannot be traded (ADR-026). Good enough.

## Privacy checklist

- **Data:** how many freezes the player holds, the week last earned, the days a freeze covered (at most 60 kept).
- **Retention:** in `player_stats`, erased with it (ADR-048), moved with a guest (ADR-014).
- **Who sees it:** the player only, and their export.
- **Minors (ADR-028):** the same; no messages outside the app.
- **Defaults:** off until the flag is on.

## Alternatives considered

- **Paid freezes** (Duolingo gems, Snapchat's restore): a coin sink, ruled out by ADR-026.
- **Rest days chosen in advance**: more to explain, and the player must act; automatic is kinder.
- **Grace hours** after midnight: hides the rule and does not cover a whole day.

## Research (written before any code)

1. **How our code does it today.** `domain/gamification/streaks.js`: next day +1, any gap resets to 1, read with
   `now` shows 0 for a gap and `atRisk` for yesterday. The streak lives in `player_stats.dailyStreak
   { current, best, lastDay }`, changed once per match by key (ADR-013.2, .6), rebuilt from history at a guest
   sign-in (ADR-013.8). The card says "Play today to keep your N-day streak" when at risk.
2. **How others do it.**
   - **Duolingo** Streak Freeze "allows you to hit pause on your streak for a day"; learners may "equip up to two
     Freezes at a time", which raised daily active learners by 0.38 % and did not lead learners to take more days
     off. Freezes are refilled with gems (Osman Mansur, Duolingo blog, 31 January 2022, read 2026-10-21). Our cap of
     2 matches; our freezes are free and earned by play instead (ADR-026).
   - **Snapchat** Snapstreaks expire after a day without Snaps, show an hourglass when "about to expire", and can be
     restored "for a limited time only" (Snapchat Support, read 2026-10-21). The hourglass is a loss-aversion cue we
     do not copy.
   - **Ethics.** Zagal, Björk and Lewis, "Dark Patterns in the Design of Games" (FDG 2013), name temporal dark
     patterns such as "playing by appointment", where the game punishes not showing up. The ICO Children's code
     standard 13 suggests nudges that support wellbeing, such as "pause and save buttons" (ico.org.uk, read
     2026-10-21). The 5Rights Foundation's "Disrupted Childhood" (2018) names streaks as persuasive design aimed at
     children.
3. **What is best for ClassiCade.** A freeze is our "pause": automatic, free, earned only by playing, capped at 2,
   and never sold. The count stays honest (frozen days add nothing). The copy states facts and never threatens a
   loss, for every age. No background job: the rule runs on read and at the next match, so old documents simply
   have 0 freezes.
