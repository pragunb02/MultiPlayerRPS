## Problem

CC-174 (the telemetry client) may only ship with the privacy page section that says what is counted, in the same
pull request or before it (EVENTS.md section 5), and the owner chose (2026-10-20, CC-174's open decision) that the
section goes live a week before the client flag ramps, with its date and no banner.

## Fix

The privacy page gains section 4a, "How we count how the site is used", with the wording EVENTS.md section 5
proposed (minus the adult tier, which ADR-034.1 did not build): what is counted, that it goes only to our own server
with no name, email, address, typed text or room code and the time to the hour only, no id for anyone, 30 days and 13
months, and the switch, which is on the page itself and in the profile's privacy settings. Section 3 lists
`cc.telemetry`, section 4 says "no third-party analytics", section 6 gives the retention, the footer says "no
third-party trackers", and the page's date is 20 October 2026, with counting starting no earlier than 27 October.
Research note: the decision itself was already recorded in ADR-034 (2026-10-18) and amended with the owner's
2026-10-20 answers. The page is checked against DATA.md by `privacyFacts.test.jsx` (CC-248).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1** Given the privacy page, when it is read, then it says what is counted, that the counts carry no id and go
  only to our own server, how long they are kept, and how to switch them off, and DATA.md says the same.

## Test

`apps/web/src/pages/LegalPages/privacyFacts.test.jsx` › "the page says it: usage counts are anonymous, on our own
server, single counts 30 days, off with the switch, GPC or DNT (CC-174)" and "the page says it: the usage counts
switch keeps only cc.telemetry in this browser (CC-174)". Both failed against the old page.
