## Research (written before any code)

The research is shared with CC-245 and written there first: [CC-245](CC-245.md), Research, sections 1.7 (how sign-up
answers today), 2.1 and 2.2 (OWASP guidance and the usual "if it's yours, log in or reset your password" pattern) and
3.6 (what is best for us).

In short:

1. Today a sign-up with an email that has an account, or that belongs to a player of the old app, answers 409
   `CONFLICT` "That email can't be used" on `details.field: "email"`. The page shows it under the email field and
   offers nothing else. The real owner, who most likely forgot they signed up, has nowhere to go.
2. OWASP wants one answer for every case. The full answer ("check your email") needs email (CC-033). Until then the
   usual middle ground is one message for every unavailable email that points the owner on: log in, or reset the
   password.
3. For us: one message, defined once, for every unavailable email (an account's or a legacy player's, in any spelling
   CC-245 folds together). "log in" links to `/login` and keeps `next`. The email never goes into a URL, so it cannot
   leak through history, logs or the `Referer` header. Password reset does not exist yet, so it is named but not
   linked.

## Why

A player who already has an account and tries to sign up again is stuck: the page says no and gives no way on.

## Scope

- In: one message for every unavailable email, the log in link that keeps `next`, unit and browser tests.
- Out: password reset (CC-033), and hiding that the email is taken (SEC-17, needs CC-033).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] One message for every unavailable email: "That email can't be used. If it's yours, log in or reset your
      password."
- [x] "log in" links to `/login`, keeping `next`. No email in any URL.
- [x] Only what exists is linked: password reset is named, not linked (missing until CC-033)
- [x] Unit test and browser test
- [x] `npm run lint`, `npm run format:check`, `npm test` pass
- [ ] Docs updated (BACKLOG status, KNOWN_ISSUES as BUG-31, PROGRESS): left to the coordinator on merge

## Plan / notes

1. The words live once, in `@classicade/shared` (`ACCOUNT_MESSAGES.EMAIL_UNAVAILABLE`). The server sends them for
   every unavailable email: the legacy check in `accountComponent.signup` and the duplicate email in
   `accountAccessor.create`.
2. The sign-up page shows its own copy with "log in" as a link (`EmailUnavailable` in `SignupPage.jsx`) when the
   answer is `CONFLICT` on the email field. A unit test pins that its text is exactly the shared message. `Field`
   now takes a node as its error, so the message can hold the link and still be the field's description for screen
   readers.
3. **Missing:** password reset. The message names it so the wording does not change when CC-033 lands; CC-033 turns
   "reset your password" into a link to its page (the same `next` rule applies, and still no email in the URL).

## Verification

1. `apps/web/src/pages/auth.test.jsx`, "an email that can't be used": the field shows the one message as its
   description and is marked invalid; the only link is "log in", to `/login?next=%2Fgames%2Fchess` with no email in
   it; clicking it opens the login page with an empty email box. These failed before the change.
2. `apps/server/src/domain/accounts/accountComponent.test.js`: every unavailable case answers the same message and
   field (an account's email, the same email in another Unicode form or in full-width letters, a legacy email in
   another case, with spaces around it, and a legacy twin).
3. Browser, `e2e/user.spec.js` "signing up with an email that has an account says so once and links to log in",
   desktop and mobile, `E2E_PORT=4969`: signs up, logs out, signs up again with the same email in capitals and with
   spaces, sees the message, clicks "log in", lands on `/login?next=%2Fgames%2Fchess`, logs in and lands on
   `/games/chess`. The players fixture checks that no email appears in any URL. 2 passed.
4. `e2e/legacy.spec.js` now expects the same full message for a legacy player's email. 4 passed.
